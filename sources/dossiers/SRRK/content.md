## 1. Profile

Scholar Rock Holding Corporation (Nasdaq: SRRK), headquartered at 301 Binney Street, 3rd Floor, Cambridge, MA 02142. Stage: late-stage biopharmaceutical company with one newly approved product. Lead program: apitegromab (brand name ISEMBYLD™), a fully human monoclonal antibody that inhibits myostatin activation, for spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) — **FDA-approved on September 11, 2026** for adults and children ≥2 years old on background SMN2-targeted therapy, making it the first muscle-targeted treatment approved for SMA. The company is now transitioning from a clinical-stage company to a commercial-stage company in the U.S., while continuing to develop apitegromab in facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) and advancing an early pipeline of other myostatin/TGFβ-pathway candidates (SRK-439, SRK-181, SRK-373, SRK-256).

## 2. One-Minute Read

- **FDA approved ISEMBYLD (apitegromab-mstn) for SMA on September 11, 2026** — ahead of the September 30, 2026 PDUFA date — making it the first and only muscle-targeted SMA treatment; U.S. commercial launch began within days of approval (8-K filed 9/14/2026, Item 7.01/8.01).
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were **$492.1 million as of June 30, 2026**, up from $367.6 million at year-end 2025, helped by $160.8 million of ATM stock sales and a new $200.0 million term loan draw in H1 2026 (10-Q balance sheet; 10-Q MD&A).
- Q2 2026 net loss was **$109.9 million** ($0.84/share), essentially flat versus a $110.0 million net loss in Q2 2025, despite the cost of launch preparation, because lower stock-based compensation partly offset higher spend (10-Q statement of operations).
- Six-month 2026 operating cash burn was **$152.4 million**, versus $155.6 million in the prior-year period — a roughly flat burn rate even as the company geared up for commercial launch (10-Q cash flow statement).
- Management now guides that existing cash will fund operations **"into the second half of 2027"** (10-Q, Liquidity/Funding Requirements) — before counting any product revenue from the September 2026 approval, which is outside the filing window reviewed here.
- Window update: a post-approval investor call on September 14, 2026 led to a same-week corrective 8-K (filed 9/15/2026) disclosing that management had misstated, during Q&A, that the FDA did not require post-marketing safety commitments — the FDA in fact required a pregnancy safety study and 15-day fracture "alert report" submissions.

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management guidance (verbatim, 10-Q "Funding Requirements," MD&A):**
> "Based on our current operating model, we expect that our existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities will enable us to fund our operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into the second half of 2027."

The 10-Q separately states the standard twelve-month going-concern assertion in Note 1: "The Company believes that its existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026 will be sufficient to allow the Company to fund its current operations through at least a period of one year after the date these financial statements are issued." This twelve-month test is a shorter, minimum-compliance horizon than the "second half of 2027" guidance window above.

| Basis | Cash & marketable securities (6/30/2026) | Quarterly use | Quarters of runway | Approx. end point |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Calculated A — operating burn | $492,088K | $76,204K (= $152,408K YTD operating cash outflow ÷ 2 quarters) | ~6.5 | About Q1 2028 |
| Calculated B — adjusted burn | $492,088K | $76,204K (no change from A) | ~6.5 | About Q1 2028 |
| Calculated C — minimum-cash-covenant adjusted | $372,088K (= $492,088K − $120,000K covenant) | $76,204K | ~4.9 | About Q2–Q3 2027 |
| Pro forma | No incremental financing proceeds identified between 6/30/2026 and the 10-Q filing date in the filings reviewed | n/a | n/a | Not applicable — see note |

Notes on the calculations:
- **Calculated A** uses the six-month 2026 operating cash outflow of $152.408 million, averaged over two quarters ($76.204 million/quarter), divided into the $492.088 million of cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities at June 30, 2026: $492.088M ÷ $76.204M ≈ 6.46 quarters (≈6.5 quarters), reaching roughly early 2028.
- **Calculated B** (operating burn plus scheduled debt repayment and lease payments) is unchanged from Calculated A because the 2026 Loan Agreement with Blue Owl is an interest-only bullet term loan maturing February 26, 2032 with no mandatory principal amortization before maturity, and operating lease cash payments are already embedded in the operating cash flow used for Calculated A.
- **Calculated C** subtracts the minimum-cash covenant under the 2026 Loan Agreement: the Company must maintain unrestricted cash of at least 60% of the aggregate outstanding Term Loan principal (unless certain milestones are met and maintained). With $200.0 million of Term Loans drawn as of the 10-Q date, the covenant floor is $120.0 million, leaving $372.088 million of "spendable" cash above the covenant, which funds roughly 4.9 quarters of operating burn (into Q2–Q3 2027).
- **Pro forma**: the only post-6/30/2026, pre-as-of-date capital-markets item identified is an August 6, 2026 prospectus supplement (filed the same day as the 10-Q) re-upsizing the Jefferies ATM program to $200.0 million of additional capacity; it replaced a November 2025 prospectus supplement and, as of its filing, no incremental proceeds under the new supplement were disclosed. The September 11, 2026 FDA approval of ISEMBYLD is a major value and future-revenue catalyst but does not itself add cash to the balance sheet as of the data reviewed, so no pro forma adjustment is made.
- The quarter-over-quarter run rate does not diverge from the six-month average by more than 20%: Q1 2026 total operating expense was $102.016 million and Q2 2026 was $108.901 million (per the Q1 and Q2 earnings press releases), both close to the $105.46 million quarterly average, so no separate sensitivity case is shown.
- Gap between guidance and calculated figures: management's "second half of 2027" guidance is longer than Calculated A/B's ~6.5-quarter (early 2028) result and Calculated C's ~4.9-quarter (mid-2027) result in slightly different directions — Calculated A/B actually implies a later end point than management's own guidance window, which suggests management's internal planning assumptions (e.g., ramping commercial/launch spend, or a more conservative burn assumption) are embedded in guidance in a way the simple historical-burn extrapolation does not capture. Investors should treat the historical-burn-based Calculated A/B as a lower bound on spending intensity, since post-approval commercial launch costs (not fully reflected in the pre-approval H1 2026 burn rate) are likely to increase cash use in H2 2026 and 2027.

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
