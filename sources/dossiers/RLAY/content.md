## 1. Profile

- **Company:** Relay Therapeutics, Inc. | **Ticker:** RLAY (Nasdaq) | **CIK:** 0001812364
- **HQ:** Cambridge, Massachusetts
- **Stage classification:** **Late stage** — lead program zovegalisib (RLY-2608) is in a global registrational **Phase 3** trial (ReDiscover-2) in HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer. The company also has mid-stage (Phase 1/2) and early (preclinical) programs.
- **Lead program one-liner:** Zovegalisib (RLY-2608), the first allosteric, pan-mutant, isoform-selective PI3Kα inhibitor, is being developed both in PI3Kα-mutated HR+/HER2- breast cancer (Phase 3 ReDiscover-2) and in PIK3CA-driven vascular anomalies (Phase 2 ReInspire), a genetic-disease indication outside oncology.

## 2. One-minute read

- **Cash position jumped to $910.9 million** (cash, cash equivalents and investments) as of June 30, 2026, up from ~$554.5M at Dec 31, 2025 (cash+investments), after a $296.8 million net follow-on offering (May 2026) and $159.2 million of net ATM proceeds in H1 2026 (10-Q, Liquidity and Capital Resources).
- **Management guidance: runway "into 2029"** — materially longer than the operating-burn-only calculation below, reflecting the large H1 2026 capital raises (10-Q, p.12).
- **Breakthrough Therapy designation (Feb 2026)** granted to zovegalisib + fulvestrant for PIK3CA-mutant HR+/HER2- breast cancer following CDK4/6 progression (10-Q narrative; 8-K Feb 26, 2026 context).
- **First vascular-anomaly data (May 19, 2026, 8-K Ex-99.1):** in the Phase 2 ReInspire trial, 60% volumetric response rate across doses (20 response-evaluable of 32 enrolled, BICR-assessed), with 89%/79% of patients reporting clinical improvement (investigator/patient-reported) at week 12 — a new, non-oncology growth driver for the pipeline.
- **R&D spend up 20% quarter-over-quarter** ($76.5M in Q2 2026 vs. $63.9M in Q2 2025) driven by zovegalisib trial costs; net loss widened to $83.7M in Q2 2026 from $70.4M in Q2 2025 (10-Q income statement).

## 6. Runway calculations

- **Management's own guidance (verbatim):** "We believe our cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $910.9 million as of June 30, 2026 will enable us to fund our operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into 2029." (10-Q, MD&A, Liquidity and Capital Resources). Elsewhere the 10-Q separately states the standard 12-month GAAP going-concern assertion: "We expect that our existing cash and cash equivalents and investments will be sufficient to fund our operations through at least the next 12 months."
- **Calculated A (operating burn basis):** H1 2026 net cash used in operating activities = $105.221M over 2 quarters = ~$52.6M/quarter average quarterly operating burn. Cash + investments at Jun 30, 2026 = $121,189 + $789,745 = $910,934 (≈$910.9M, consistent with MD&A). $910.9M ÷ $52.6M/quarter ≈ **17.3 quarters (≈4.3 years), i.e., about Q4 2030.**
- **Calculated B (adjusted burn, debt/lease):** RLAY discloses no outstanding debt in the 10-Q's balance sheet. Operating lease liabilities (current $4.0M + non-current $26.6M = $30.7M total) are a non-cash/contractual obligation already reflected through operating cash flow for lease payments; no separate principal repayment schedule for debt is disclosed in filings reviewed, so Calculated B = Calculated A (no adjustment identified; "not disclosed in filings reviewed" for a distinct debt amortization schedule).
- **Pro forma:** No financing event closed after the June 30, 2026 balance-sheet date was identified in 8-Ks filed through Oct 26, 2026 (none filed in that window), so no pro forma adjustment is made; pro forma = Calculated A.
- **Sensitivity note:** Calculated A's quarterly run rate ($52.6M/quarter average) differs from the Q2 2026 standalone operating expense run rate implied by the income statement (Total opex $91.2M for the quarter, though this is expense not cash burn; actual quarterly operating cash outflow is not separately broken out in the 10-Q beyond the six-month figure). Readers should treat the $52.6M/quarter as a 6-month average, not a single-quarter actual.
- **Funding sources not counted in the runway math:**
  - Remaining capacity under the August 2024 Sales Agreement (ATM) with TD Securities: $250.0M aggregate authorized, $163.0M gross sold as of Jun 30, 2026 (net $159.2M), leaving approximately $87.0M in gross ATM capacity undrawn.
  - Up to $485.0 million in additional regulatory/commercial milestone payments plus tiered royalties owed by Elevar Therapeutics under the December 2024 lirafugratinib licensing agreement (not contracted-and-dated cash, so excluded from runway per the skill's rule; upside only).
  - No warrants outstanding or separate shelf-registration dollar capacity was identified in the 10-Q; "not disclosed in filings reviewed" for any additional shelf beyond the described Sales Agreement.
