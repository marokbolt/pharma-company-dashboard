# Kymera Therapeutics, Inc. (KYMR) — Investor Tearsheet
*Prepared 2026-10-26. Source: SEC EDGAR filings (CIK 0001815442). Latest 10-Q: quarter ended June 30, 2026, filed August 5, 2026.*

---

## 1. Profile

- **Company:** Kymera Therapeutics, Inc.
- **Ticker:** KYMR (Nasdaq Global Market)
- **HQ:** 500 North Beacon Street, 4th Floor, Watertown, Massachusetts 02472
- **Stage:** Clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company
- **Lead programs:** KT-621 (oral STAT6 degrader) in Phase 2b for atopic dermatitis (BROADEN2) and asthma (BREADTH); KT-579 (oral IRF5 degrader) in Phase 1
- **Partnered programs:** KT-485/SAR447971 (oral IRAK4 degrader) partnered with **Sanofi**, in Phase 1 (Sanofi-led); KT-200 (oral CDK2 molecular glue degrader) licensed to **Gilead Sciences** following Gilead's April 2026 option exercise

Kymera is a clinical-stage targeted protein degradation (TPD) company developing oral small-molecule degraders, primarily for immunological diseases, with two of its three named clinical programs economically shared with large pharma partners (Sanofi and Gilead) via upfront, milestone and royalty arrangements.

## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **Cash runway guided into 2029**, with $1,504.9 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026 (down from $1,742.8 million at Dec 31, 2025).
2. **KT-621 BROADEN2 Phase 2b (atopic dermatitis) enrollment completed ~6 months ahead of schedule** (announced June 25, 2026), pulling the expected topline readout forward from mid-2027 to **year-end 2026**; Phase 3 initiation in AD now targeted by mid-2027.
3. **$65.0 million of collaboration revenue in Q2 2026** (vs. $11.5 million in Q2 2025), driven by a $45.0 million Gilead option-exercise fee (KT-200) and a $20.0 million Sanofi milestone (first patient dosed in KT-485/SAR447971 Phase 1).
4. **Net loss narrowed to $61.2 million in Q2 2026** from $76.6 million in Q2 2025, largely on higher collaboration revenue, even as R&D spend rose to $119.5 million (from $78.4 million) on STAT6 program investment.
5. **Leadership transition in the clinical organization**: new CMO Terence Rooney, MD appointed July 27, 2026 (succeeding retiring Jared Gollob, MD); COO Jeremy Chadwick resigned June 5, 2026; Felix J. Baker, PhD became Board Chairman in June 2026 (succeeding Bruce Booth).

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, from Q2 2026 8-K Exhibit 99.1, August 5, 2026):**
> "Well-capitalized with $1.5 billion in cash as of June 30, 2026, and runway into 2029"

**Verbatim from 10-Q MD&A (filed Aug 5, 2026):**
> "As of June 30, 2026, we had cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $1,505 million. We believe the existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities on hand will be sufficient to fund our operations into 2029, beyond multiple clinical inflection points in our pipeline."

**Calculated A — operating burn basis:**
- YTD (6-month) net cash used in operating activities: $142,905 thousand
- Average quarterly operating cash outflow: $142,905 / 2 = **$71,453 thousand/quarter**
- Cash + marketable securities (current + non-current) as of June 30, 2026: $124,477 + $566,508 + $813,901 = **$1,504,886 thousand**
- Runway = $1,504,886 / $71,453 ≈ **21.1 quarters ≈ 5.3 years from June 30, 2026 → roughly late 2031**

This mechanical calculation runs well beyond management's "into 2029" guidance. The gap is explained by the fact that H1 2026 operating cash outflow was unusually suppressed by one-time, non-recurring collaboration cash inflows (the $45M Gilead option-exercise fee and other milestone-related receivables/deferred-revenue movements) netted against operating costs, and because management's own guidance presumably assumes materially higher spend going forward (Phase 3 AD trial initiation targeted by mid-2027, continued BREADTH asthma Phase 2b costs, and no further embedded assumption of large non-dilutive milestone receipts). Investors should treat Calculated A as a conservative/optimistic mechanical extrapolation of a currently milestone-boosted burn rate, not as a reliable independent forecast, and should weight management's guidance more heavily.

**Calculated B — debt/lease basis:** Kymera carries only finance lease liabilities ($1,577K current + $840K non-current = $2,417K total) and operating lease liabilities ($12,119K current + $64,022K non-current = $76,141K total); there is no term debt or credit facility disclosed in the filings reviewed. These lease obligations are not material to a separate debt-service-driven runway calculation and are already reflected as cash outflows in the operating cash flow statement (financing activities reflect only $861K of finance-lease payments YTD). No separate "Calculated B" debt-driven runway is applicable — "not disclosed in filings reviewed" for any debt facility.

**Pro forma / post-balance-sheet financing:** No equity or debt financing was announced between the 10-Q filing date (August 5, 2026) and today (October 26, 2026) in the filings reviewed. The only subsequent event disclosed is a related-party sublease of office/lab space to Merida Biosciences, Inc. (not a financing event). No pro forma adjustment to the runway is warranted.

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- **ATM / shelf facility:** New $500.0 million Cowen "at-the-market" sales agreement entered February 26, 2026 (replacing the terminated Jefferies $300.0 million ATM, which was never drawn, and the earlier $250.0 million Cowen ATM, of which only ~$50 million gross was sold historically). As of June 30, 2026, $0 has been sold under the new Cowen agreement — fully undrawn.
- **Debt facilities:** None disclosed.
- **Warrants/options:** Proceeds from stock option exercises ($32,577K YTD) and ESPP ($1,472K YTD) are inflows already reflected in financing activities, but are not guaranteed recurring sources and are excluded from the burn-based runway calculation above.
- **Partner milestones:** Kymera remains eligible for up to $955 million in additional Sanofi IRAK4 development/commercial milestones and up to $665.0 million in additional Gilead CDK2 milestones (plus tiered royalties from both), none of which are assumed in the runway calculation.
