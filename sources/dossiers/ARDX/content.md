## 1. Profile

Ardelyx, Inc. is a Delaware-incorporated, Waltham, Massachusetts headquartered **commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company** (CIK 0001437402; Nasdaq: ARDX). It is not a clinical-stage company by the skill's Early/Mid/Late rubric — it has **two approved, commercially marketed products in the U.S.**: IBSRELA (tenapanor) for IBS-C, and XPHOZAH (tenapanor) for serum phosphorus reduction in CKD patients on dialysis. Separately from its commercial business, its clinical pipeline is **early-to-mid stage**: a Phase 3 trial (ACCEL) is underway for a new indication (CIC) of its already-approved drug tenapanor, pediatric trials of IBSRELA are ongoing, and RDX10531 (a next-generation NHE3 inhibitor) is in IND-enabling (preclinical) studies. Lead program one-liner: IBSRELA and XPHOZAH (both tenapanor, an NHE3 inhibitor) are approved and generating revenue; the next pipeline catalyst is Phase 3 ACCEL topline data (tenapanor in chronic idiopathic constipation), expected second half of 2027.

## 2. One-minute read

- Q2 2026 total product revenue was $118.1 million, up 31% year-over-year (IBSRELA $86.2M, +33% YoY; XPHOZAH $31.9M, +27% YoY) — the largest quarterly revenue in company history (Ex-99.1, 8/6/2026 earnings release).
- Despite record revenue, management cut full-year 2026 IBSRELA revenue guidance to $350–$370 million (from a higher prior range) due to "significantly increased utilization management processes from payors"; XPHOZAH FY2026 guidance was reiterated at $110–$120 million, but XPHOZAH's long-term guidance was withdrawn entirely pending reassessment of market dynamics (Ex-99.1).
- Net loss narrowed to $16.7 million in Q2 2026 (vs. $19.1 million in Q2 2025), and cash plus short-term investments grew to $281.8 million as of June 30, 2026 (vs. $264.7 million at December 31, 2025), even after Ardelyx drew an incremental $50 million term loan from SLR Investment Corp. on June 29, 2026 (8-K, 7/2/2026; 10-Q).
- In the review window, long-term debt rose to $251.8 million (from $202.8 million at year-end 2025) following the $50 million Term F Loan draw, which matures July 1, 2030 and carries SOFR + 4.55% (floor 3.5% SOFR) interest; total future principal due on term loans was $260.8 million as of June 30, 2026 (10-Q, Note 8).
- Window event: Chief Commercial Officer Eric Foster resigned effective August 28, 2026, to join a pre-commercial company; the company stated the departure was not due to any disagreement (8-K filed 8/17/2026, Item 8.01). This is a management-change item, not thesis-changing on its own.

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, 10-Q MD&A, "Funding Requirements"):** "Based on our current operating model, we believe our available cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments as of June 30, 2026 will be sufficient to fund our planned operations for at least a period of one year from the issuance of these financial statements." This is the standard US GAAP going-concern 12-month test, not a specific calendar-quarter guidance horizon; no further-out horizon (e.g., "into 2029") was given in filings reviewed.

Note: because Ardelyx's operating cash burn is modest relative to cash on hand and the company is moving toward profitability (per CEO commentary, "moving towards profitability in 2027"), a strict cash-depletion "runway" framing is less central to the thesis than for a pre-revenue biotech; the calculations below are the requested mechanical output.

| Basis | Formula | Quarterly use ($) | Quarters of runway | Approx. end point |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Calculated A — operating burn | Cash + short-term investments at 6/30/26 ($281,830) ÷ average quarterly operating cash outflow (6-mo. operating cash used $38,636 ÷ 2 quarters = $19,318/quarter) | $19,318 | ~14.6 quarters | ~Q1 2030 |
| Calculated B — adjusted burn (+ debt/lease service) | Calculated A burn + scheduled interest-only debt service is already interest-only through 2030 (no principal amortization in the window) and operating lease payments (~$897K remainder of 2026, ~$1,836K 2027 per lease schedule); adding an estimated pro-rated lease payment of roughly $230/quarter to the $19,318 base gives ~$19,548/quarter | $19,548 | ~14.4 quarters | ~Q4 2029 |
| Pro forma | Add the $50.0 million Term F Loan draw (June 29, 2026) on a gross basis — already reflected in the 6/30/26 cash and short-term investment balance of $281,830, so no further pro forma adjustment is needed for financings closed after the balance-sheet date; no other post-period financing was identified in filings reviewed through October 23, 2026 | n/a | n/a | Same as Calculated A/B, since the $50M draw predates the balance-sheet date |

**Latest-quarter run rate sensitivity:** Q2 2026 operating cash burn alone is not separately broken out from the six-month figure in the filing (only the six-month cash flow statement is presented); therefore a quarter-only sensitivity cannot be computed and is marked "not disclosed in filings reviewed."

**Interpretation:** The calculated mechanical runway (roughly 14–15 quarters, out past 2029) is far longer than management's conservative 12-month going-concern statement, because Ardelyx is a commercial-stage company with declining net operating cash burn ($38.6 million used in 1H2026 vs. $63.8 million in 1H2025) and growing product revenue, not a pre-revenue biotech spending down a fixed cash pile. Management's 12-month language reflects standard going-concern disclosure practice, not a company-specific risk signal.
