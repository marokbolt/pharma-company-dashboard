## 1. Profile

- **Company:** Heron Therapeutics, Inc.

- **Stage classification:** Commercial-stage (fully commercial; four FDA-approved products, no clinical-stage pipeline candidates disclosed in filings reviewed — the company's "pipeline" activity in this window is limited to lifecycle/label and reimbursement management of existing products, not new investigational compounds)
- **Lead program / franchise one-liner:** Four approved acute-care and oncology-supportive-care injectable products — ZYNRELEF® (bupivacaine/meloxicam, postsurgical pain), APONVIE® (IV aprepitant, PONV prevention), CINVANTI® (IV aprepitant, CINV prevention) and SUSTOL® (extended-release granisetron, CINV prevention, being discontinued) — sold through a commercial sales force, with no new clinical pipeline disclosed.

## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **Q2 2026 total net revenue was $37.7 million**, up 9% sequentially from Q1 2026 but only up 1.3% year-over-year ($37.7M vs. $37.2M); six-month revenue of $72.4 million was down 4.9% year-over-year, driven by a CINVANTI patent-litigation loss.
2. **Going concern / liquidity is tight:** cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were $42.7 million as of June 30, 2026 (down from $46.6M at Dec 31, 2025), and the company disclosed explicit "Liquidity and Going Concern" language in the 10-Q, conditioned on a lender waiver obtained days before filing.
3. **Adverse patent ruling on CINVANTI** (largest product, ~58% of Q2 revenue): on June 1, 2026 the U.S. District Court for Delaware held key CINVANTI patent claims invalid; Heron is appealing to the Federal Circuit but withdrew full-year 2026 guidance as a direct result, citing inability to forecast generic entry timing.
4. **Lender required an emergency $17.5 million debt paydown:** the August 7, 2026 Fourth Amendment to the Hercules Capital Working Capital Facility Agreement waived Q2 covenant breaches in exchange for a $13.5 million immediate principal prepayment plus up to $4.0 million more due by September 15, 2026, and eliminated $40 million of previously available future tranches.
5. **Going-concern-adjacent corporate stress signals stacking up:** Nasdaq minimum-bid-price deficiency notice (June 25, 2026, cure deadline December 22, 2026), management stated it is "considering strategic alternatives," SUSTOL being wound down (notified FDA June 15, 2026 it will stop sales after September 30, 2026), and stockholders' equity fell to just $6.9 million (from $14.3 million at year-end 2025).

## 4. Clinical Trial Results Summary

No new clinical trial readouts were disclosed in the window (April 1 – October 23, 2026). The filings reference only the historical, previously reported Phase 3 programs that supported the already-approved labels:

- **ZYNRELEF Phase 3 program** (historical, pre-window): demonstrated statistically significant reduction in pain and significantly increased proportion of patients requiring no opioids through 72 hours post-surgery vs. bupivacaine solution (comparator-controlled). Exact population sizes, p-values and cut-off dates are not disclosed in the 10-Q/8-Ks reviewed (filing refers to the trials only in summary form); for the full trial data, the original NDA-supporting Phase 3 publications would need to be reviewed (not covered by this filing set).
- **SUSTOL global Phase 3 program** (historical, pre-window): two large guideline-based studies in >2,000 cancer patients evaluating acute (0–24h) and delayed (24–120h) CINV prevention efficacy and safety; no new data cut disclosed in this window.
- **CINVANTI bioequivalence data** (historical): CINVANTI shown bioequivalent to EMEND® IV with fewer adverse events in healthy volunteers; no new data in this window.

No new efficacy/safety trial results were reported for the quarter; the only trial-adjacent news in the window was legal/patent (CINVANTI litigation outcome, section 3 above), not a clinical result.

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, from 10-Q Note 1 "Liquidity and Going Concern"):**
> "As of June 30, 2026, we had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $42.7 million... Subsequent to the receipt of the Fourth Amendment to the Working Capital Facility Agreement and based on our current operating plan and projections, management believes that the Company's cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments will be sufficient to meet the Company's anticipated cash requirements for a period of at least one year from the issuance of the condensed consolidated financial statements included in this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q. We have based this estimate on assumptions that may prove wrong, and we could utilize our available capital resources sooner than we currently expect."

Note: this "at least one year" guidance is explicitly conditioned on the going-concern disclosure and lender waiver; the filing states the financial statements are "prepared assuming we will continue as a going concern."

**Calculated A — Operating burn basis:**
- H1 2026 net cash used in operating activities = $3.753 million over 6 months → average quarterly operating burn ≈ $1.9 million/quarter (very low, reflecting the business being near cash-flow breakeven on an operating basis before debt service).
- Cash + short-term investments at 6/30/2026 = $42.7 million.
- At the H1 2026 average burn rate alone, operating cash would last roughly 42.7 / (3.753/2) ≈ 22+ quarters (>5 years) — but this measure is materially misleading on its own because it excludes the $17.5 million mandatory debt paydown described below and ignores the likely step-down in CINVANTI revenue from the adverse patent ruling, which the company itself flagged as the reason it withdrew guidance.

**Calculated B — Including mandatory debt service / lease and the post-balance-sheet $17.5 million paydown (pro forma):**
- Pro forma cash position after the $13.5 million prepayment already made upon the Fourth Amendment's effectiveness (early August 2026) and before the contingent second prepayment of up to $4.0 million due by September 15, 2026: approximately $42.7M − $13.5M − (up to $4.0M) ≈ **$25.2 million to $29.2 million** of available liquidity shortly after the quarter end, subject to further reduction for End of Term Fees ($661,500 plus up to $196,000) and continued operating burn.
- Adding back modest quarterly operating cash burn (~$1.9M/quarter) and fixed interest cash payments (H1 2026 interest paid of $5.3 million, i.e., ~$2.7M/quarter), pro forma liquidity of ~$25–29 million would support roughly 3–5 quarters of combined operating and debt-service cash needs even before accounting for any further revenue deterioration from the CINVANTI ruling — materially shorter than the "at least one year" management statement would suggest in isolation, and consistent with why the company flagged going-concern risk and is "considering strategic alternatives."

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- Remaining tranches under the original Working Capital Facility (tranche 2 of $20.0 million and tranche 3 of $20.0 million) were **eliminated** by the August 7, 2026 Fourth Amendment — no longer available, explicitly removed as a funding source.
- No active ATM (at-the-market) equity program or shelf registration drawdown was identified in the 10-Q or 8-Ks reviewed (not disclosed in filings reviewed).
- Outstanding Lender Warrants (297,619 shares at $1.68/share strike, 7-year term from 2023 issuance) — dilutive but immaterial dollar proceeds if exercised.
- 2031 Convertible Notes ($35.0 million principal, convertible at $1.80/share into up to 19,444,444 shares) — a potential source of equity conversion rather than cash, and does not add liquidity unless additional notes are issued.
- Remaining 70,012 shares of Series A convertible preferred stock not yet converted (held back due to beneficial-ownership limits) — dilutive overhang, not a cash source.
- September 23, 2026 Long Grove/Capstone Development and License Agreement (post-10-Q 8-K) — a new licensing arrangement with milestone and royalty obligations owed BY Heron (not an incoming funding source); full economic terms not disclosed in the 8-K (to be filed as an exhibit to the Q3 2026 10-Q).
- Potential outcome of "strategic alternatives" process referenced by the CEO — undefined, no transaction announced as of October 23, 2026.
