## 1. Profile

ADMA Biologics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ADMA), headquartered in Ramsey, New Jersey (with manufacturing/plasma operations in Boca Raton, Florida), is a **commercial-stage, profitable specialty biopharmaceutical company** — not a clinical-stage biotech. ADMA manufactures and markets three FDA-approved plasma-derived biologics: **ASCENIV™** (immune globulin intravenous, human – slra 10% liquid, for primary humoral immunodeficiency/PI), **BIVIGAM®** (immune globulin intravenous, human, for PI), and **NABI-HB®** (hepatitis B immune globulin, human). ASCENIV is the company's primary growth driver. The only pipeline asset is **SG-001**, a pre-clinical, early-stage investigational hyperimmune globulin targeting *S. pneumoniae*, which is pre-IND (not yet in human trials).

## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **ADMA is solidly profitable and growing:** Q2 2026 total revenue was $124.4 million (+2% y/y), driven by ASCENIV revenue of $102.9 million (+24% y/y), while BIVIGAM revenue declined but stabilized sequentially. GAAP net income was $37.8 million (+11% y/y) and Adjusted EBITDA was $61.8 million (+22% y/y).
2. **Management reiterated full-year 2026 guidance** of $530–560 million total revenue, $170–200 million Adjusted Net Income, and $265–300 million Adjusted EBITDA (as of the August 5, 2026 Q2 release) — but this followed a **withdrawal of ADMA's previously issued long-term (multi-year) guidance** on May 6, 2026, due to "evolving U.S. Plasma Products and IG market competitive dynamics," alongside tighter near-term FY2026 figures issued that same day; the FY2026 range itself has been maintained since.
3. **The company is aggressively returning capital to shareholders**, not preserving cash for a going-concern runway: a $125 million JPMorgan accelerated share repurchase (ASR) was announced March 2, 2026 as part of a ~$200 million 2026 capital-return target (against a $500 million total authorization); through June 30, 2026, ADMA had repurchased ~13.8 million shares (~5.3% of shares outstanding) year-to-date, funded by organic operating cash flow — this is the single most consequential capital-allocation story in the window, not a dilution risk.
4. **ADMA faced a short-seller attack**: on March 24, 2026, Culper Research published a report alleging channel-stuffing and competitive weakness in ASCENIV; ADMA issued a detailed rebuttal on March 27, 2026 (8-K Item 7.01/8.01), disclosing distributor inventory and pull-through data to refute the allegations. This is a disclosed event investors should be aware of even though the company's own financial results in the window do not show the deterioration alleged.
5. **The only pipeline asset, SG-001 (pre-clinical, anti-S. pneumoniae hyperimmune globulin), remains pre-IND**, with a pre-IND meeting package submission to FDA targeted by year-end 2026 and conformance lot production planned for 2H 2026; management frames this as a long-dated ($300–500 million market opportunity) optionality item, not a near-term catalyst. Separately, ASCENIV's label was expanded on May 4, 2026 to include pediatric patients 2 years and older (previously restricted to age 12+), a completed regulatory/commercial catalyst within the window.

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Not applicable in the classic burn-rate sense.** ADMA is solidly cash-flow-positive and profitable: 1H 2026 operating cash flow was +$87.8 million and GAAP net income was +$83.2 million. There is no burn, no going-concern risk, and no near-term financing need disclosed. The relevant capital-allocation analysis is capital return and leverage, not runway:

- **Cash position:** $136.0 million at June 30, 2026, up from $87.6 million at December 31, 2025 (+$48.4 million, or +55%, in six months) despite $155.2 million of share repurchases in the same period — funded by operating cash flow plus a new $125 million draw on a JPMorgan revolving credit facility.
- **Debt:** Long-term debt rose from $69.3 million (Dec 31, 2025) to $192.8 million (Jun 30, 2026), plus $3.75 million current portion, reflecting the $125 million JPM revolving facility draw used substantially to fund the accelerated share repurchase program. Net debt (total debt minus cash) was approximately $60.6 million at June 30, 2026 (debt of $196.6 million gross less $136.0 million cash), a modest leverage level against $167.1 million of trailing-twelve-month-equivalent income from operations (1H 2026 annualized ≈ $221.4 million), though a precise trailing-twelve-month figure was not independently calculated from filings reviewed beyond the 1H figures shown above.
- **Management commentary on cash generation:** Management stated in the Q1 2026 earnings release (May 6, 2026) that the company ended Q1 2026 "with net leverage below 0.5x, driven by robust operating cash flow of $58 million during the quarter," and reiterated confidence in meeting or exceeding FY2026 guidance in the Q2 2026 release (August 5, 2026).
- **Capital return program, not a runway concern:** The $200 million 2026 share-repurchase target (against a $500 million total board authorization) is the dominant capital-allocation story. Through June 30, 2026, ADMA had repurchased ~13.8 million shares (~5.3% of shares outstanding) year-to-date; the $125 million JPMorgan ASR (announced March 2, 2026) was a major component, settled using an upfront payment with final share count true-up based on VWAP during the ASR term.
- **Upside/contingent items not in any cash figure above:** The SG-001 program is pre-clinical and self-funded from operating cash flow; no material near-term cash outlay beyond ordinary R&D ($8.6 million in 1H 2026) was disclosed for it. The Culper Research short-seller allegations (March 24, 2026) did not result in any disclosed cash outflow, litigation reserve, or restatement in the filings reviewed.
