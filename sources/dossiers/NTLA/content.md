## 1. Profile

**Company:** Intellia Therapeutics, Inc.
**Ticker:** NTLA (Nasdaq Global Market)
**CIK:** 0001652130
**HQ:** 40 Erie Street, Suite 130, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139 (per the 10-Q cover page; a September 4, 2026 8-K lists a changed principal office of 400 Technology Square, Suite 100, Cambridge, MA 02139)
**Filer type:** U.S. domestic filer, Large Accelerated Filer, files Forms 10-K/10-Q (confirmed from `category` field in EDGAR submissions JSON and the filing history, which contains standard 10-K/10-Q/8-K forms rather than 20-F/6-K foreign-private-issuer forms)
**Stage classification:** Clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company. Intellia's own wholly-owned/lead pipeline (lonvoguran ziclumeran "lonvo-z," formerly NTLA-2002; nexiguran ziclumeran "nex-z," formerly NTLA-2001) is clinical-stage (Phase 3) with no approved products and no product revenue. **Important clarification:** Intellia has **no economic interest in CASGEVY** (exa-cel), the approved CRISPR/Cas9 gene-edited therapy marketed by Vertex Pharmaceuticals and developed with CRISPR Therapeutics AG (ticker CRSP) — a company frequently confused with Intellia because both are CRISPR-based gene-editing biotechs. A full-text search of Intellia's latest 10-Q and 10-K for "Vertex" and "CASGEVY" returned zero hits; Intellia's collaboration revenue derives solely from its arrangements with Regeneron Pharmaceuticals (technology collaboration, ATTR co-development/co-promotion, and a now-ended hemophilia B co-funding arrangement) and immaterial amounts from AvenCell, Kyverna and ONK license agreements. (Source: full-text search of 10-Q filed 2026-08-06 and 10-K filed 2026-02-26; Note 7, "Collaborations and Other Arrangements," 10-Q.)
**Lead program one-liner:** Lonvo-z, a one-time in vivo CRISPR therapy inactivating the KLKB1 gene, has a BLA accepted by the FDA with Priority Review (PDUFA date March 10, 2027) for hereditary angioedema (HAE), following positive Phase 3 HAELO data (87% reduction in mean monthly attacks vs. placebo, p<0.0001); nex-z, an in vivo CRISPR therapy targeting TTR for ATTR amyloidosis, is in two ongoing Phase 3 trials (MAGNITUDE in ATTR-CM, MAGNITUDE-2 in ATTRv-PN) partnered with Regeneron.

## 2. One-minute read

- **Lonvo-z BLA accepted with Priority Review; PDUFA date March 10, 2027** — FDA will not hold an advisory committee meeting; if approved, lonvo-z would be the first in vivo CRISPR-based therapy and first one-time HAE treatment. (Source: 8-K filed 2026-09-08, Exhibit 99.1 press release.)
- **Cash runway guided "at least into 2028"** — management's own Outlook statement in the Q2 2026 10-Q, based on $628.4 million of cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026, the $194.6 million net proceeds of the April 2026 follow-on equity offering, and Regeneron cost-reimbursement funding — explicitly excluding any lonvo-z commercial revenue. (Source: 10-Q filed 2026-08-06, MD&A "Outlook" section.)
- **$628.4 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026** vs. $605.1 million at December 31, 2025, despite a net loss of $202.9 million for the six months ended June 30, 2026 — supported by $194.6 million in follow-on equity net proceeds and $33.6 million from at-the-market sales during the period. (Source: 10-Q Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets and Statements of Cash Flows.)
- **New $400 million non-dilutive OrbiMed credit facility entered into September 4, 2026** (after the 10-Q quarter-end) — $75 million funded at closing, with up to $225 million more available upon lonvo-z regulatory/revenue milestones and an uncommitted $100 million more subject to mutual agreement; 5-year maturity (September 4, 2031); interest at the greater of 3.00% or 1-month SOFR, plus a 6.15% margin. (Source: 8-K filed 2026-09-04, Items 1.01/2.03 and Exhibit 99.1.)
- **Net loss of $106.6 million in Q2 2026** (vs. $101.3 million in Q2 2025) on collaboration revenue of only $7.7 million (down from $14.2 million), driven by lower Regeneron-related revenue; R&D expense fell to $82.6 million from $97.0 million as the company focused spend on lonvo-z/nex-z and prepares for a planned 1H 2027 U.S. commercial launch of lonvo-z. (Source: 10-Q Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations and Comprehensive Loss.)

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim):** "Based on our research and development plans and our expectations related to the progress of our programs, we expect that our cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026, the net proceeds from our April 2026 equity offering, as well as research and cost reimbursement funding from our collaboration agreements, will enable us to fund our ongoing operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements at least into 2028, excluding any potential milestone payments or extension fees that could be earned and distributed under our collaboration agreements, any strategic use of capital not currently in the base case planning assumptions and any potential commercial revenues from lonvo-z." (Source: 10-Q filed 2026-08-06, Item 2 MD&A, "Outlook.") The prior 10-K (filed 2026-02-26) guided to "into the second half of 2027" as of December 31, 2025.

**Calculated A — operating burn basis:**
- Six-month net cash used in operating activities (six months ended June 30, 2026): $200,459 thousand
- Number of quarters in the YTD period: 2
- Average quarterly operating cash outflow = $200,459 / 2 = **$100,229.5 thousand/quarter**
- Cash + marketable securities (current + noncurrent) as of June 30, 2026 = $106,128 + $331,631 + $190,595 = **$628,354 thousand**
- Quarters of runway = $628,354 / $100,229.5 = **6.27 quarters** (≈18.8 months)
- From June 30, 2026, 18.8 months forward ≈ **mid-to-late January 2028** — this lands within management's "at least into 2028" guidance, serving as a rough cross-check.

**Calculated B — adding scheduled debt/lease payments:**
- As of June 30, 2026, scheduled future operating lease cash payments on leases already commenced: Remainder of 2026 $13,410K; 2027 $24,315K; 2028 $13,813K; 2029 $10,787K; 2030 $11,108K; thereafter $13,391K (total undiscounted $86,824K; these payments are already embedded in the historical operating cash flow run-rate used in Calculated A, since current leases were already being paid during the six-month period).
- However, the Tech Square Lease (~125,000 sq. ft., base rent $108.00/sq. ft./year plus 3% annual escalation, ≈$13.5 million/year once fully rent-commencing) had **not yet commenced** as of June 30, 2026 (expected to commence Q3 2026, with the Company's obligation to pay rent starting December 2026) and is **excluded** from the $86,824K table above and from the historical burn rate — this is a new, not-yet-reflected cash outflow. (Source: 10-Q Note 9, "Leases.")
- The OrbiMed $400 million credit facility (entered into September 4, 2026, after the 10-Q quarter-end) carries interest at the greater of 3.00% or 1-month SOFR, plus a 6.15% margin, payable monthly, on the $75 million drawn at closing. Using an illustrative current 1-month SOFR of ~4.3% (not provided in the filings; stated here only as an assumption for illustration), the effective rate would be ~10.45%, implying incremental annual cash interest of roughly $75,000K × 10.45% ≈ $7,838K/year (~$1,960K/quarter). This is a **new** cash outflow not reflected in the six-month 2026 burn rate used in Calculated A, since the facility did not exist until September 2026.
- Approximate incremental quarterly cash outflow once the Tech Square Lease rent and OrbiMed interest are both running: Tech Square ≈ $13,500K/4 ≈ $3,375K/quarter + OrbiMed interest ≈ $1,960K/quarter ≈ **$5,335K/quarter** additional (illustrative estimate; the actual incremental amount is not separately quantified in the filings reviewed and this estimate should not be treated as filed guidance).
- Adjusted quarterly burn (Calculated B) ≈ $100,229.5K + $5,335K ≈ **$105,565K/quarter**
- Quarters of runway (Calculated B) = $628,354 / $105,565 ≈ **5.95 quarters** (≈17.9 months) from June 30, 2026 ≈ **mid-December 2027**

**Pro forma — adjusting for post-balance-sheet financing (8-Ks reviewed):**
- Add OrbiMed initial term loan draw of $75,000K funded at closing on September 4, 2026 (ignoring any intervening Q3 2026 operating burn between June 30 and September 4, 2026, which is not yet disclosed in a filed financial statement as of this review): Pro forma cash + investments ≈ $628,354 + $75,000 = **$703,354 thousand**
- Quarters of runway, pro forma cash ÷ Calculated B quarterly burn = $703,354 / $105,565 ≈ **6.66 quarters** (≈20.0 months) from June 30, 2026 ≈ **late February/March 2028**
