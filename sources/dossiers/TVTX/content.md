## 1. Profile

Travere Therapeutics, Inc. is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered at 3611 Valley Centre Drive, Suite 300, San Diego, CA 92130, incorporated in Delaware, trading on Nasdaq as TVTX. **Stage classification:** Commercial — the company has an approved, marketed product family (FILSPARI® (sparsentan) for IgA nephropathy and FSGS, plus legacy tiopronin products) generating real product revenue, alongside a clinical pipeline led by pegtibatinase (Phase 3, classical homocystinuria) and a newly in-licensed asset, civorebrutinib (preclinical/early-stage BTK inhibitor for immune-mediated kidney diseases). Travere focuses exclusively on rare kidney diseases.

## 2. One-minute read

- **FILSPARI U.S. net product sales grew 96% year-over-year to $141.1 million in Q2 2026** (vs. $71.9 million in Q2 2025), driven by continued IgA nephropathy uptake and a new FSGS launch; total U.S. net product sales (FILSPARI + tiopronin) were $161.4 million in Q2 2026 vs. $94.8 million in Q2 2025. *(Source: 8-K Ex-99.1, Aug 4, 2026 press release.)*
- **FDA approved FILSPARI for FSGS in April 2026** (to reduce proteinuria in patients 8+ without nephrotic syndrome), making it the first and only FDA-approved medicine for FSGS, with an addressable U.S. population management estimates at >30,000 patients; the company received 2,012 new patient start forms (PSFs) in Q2 2026 reflecting both FSGS launch and ongoing IgAN growth. *(Source: 8-K Ex-99.1.)*
- **Net loss widened to $34.8 million in Q2 2026** (vs. $12.8 million in Q2 2025), driven by a one-time $40.0 million non-cash inducement expense tied to repurchasing $221 million of the 2029 convertible notes; on a non-GAAP basis the company was close to breakeven with an adjusted operating income of $30.0 million in Q2 2026 (vs. $12.0 million in Q2 2025). *(Source: 10-Q R4; 8-K Ex-99.1 non-GAAP reconciliation.)*
- **The company refinanced its convertible debt in Q2 2026**: issued $525.0 million of new 0.5% convertible senior notes due 2032 and used proceeds to repurchase $221 million face value of its 2.25% convertible notes due 2029 for $350.9 million including premium, netting roughly $158 million of new cash; convertible debt on the balance sheet rose from $311.7 million (Dec 31, 2025) to $602.8 million (June 30, 2026). *(Source: 10-Q R2, R6; 8-K Ex-99.1.)*
- **Pipeline expanded via in-licensing**: on June 1, 2026 Travere signed an exclusive license and collaboration agreement with Everest Medicines for civorebrutinib (EVER001), a BTK inhibitor, for territories excluding China/parts of SE Asia, paying a $112.5 million upfront fee (paid July 2026, a subsequent event) plus up to ~$1.03 billion in milestones; separately, the pivotal Phase 3 HARMONY study of pegtibatinase in classical homocystinuria continues to enroll, with topline data expected 2H 2027. A CEO transition was also announced September 21, 2026 (Eric Dube to step down effective December 1, 2026; Bradley Campbell, ex-Amicus Therapeutics CEO, named successor). *(Source: 8-K June 2, 2026; 8-K Sept 21, 2026; 8-K Ex-99.1.)*

## 6. Runway calculations

Travere is not a classic burn-rate story: operating cash flow was **positive** for the six months ended June 30, 2026 (+$17.7 million), and the company has an approved, growing product franchise (FILSPARI). Classic "quarters of runway" burn math is less relevant to the going-concern question here than for a pre-revenue biotech — but cash plus debt dynamics still matter because of the convertible-debt refinancing and recent in-licensing payment.

- **Cash position:** $117.7 million cash and cash equivalents plus $371.4 million marketable debt securities = **$489.2 million cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities** as of June 30, 2026 (company-reported figure in the Aug 4, 2026 press release; matches 10-Q balance sheet sum of 117,735 + 371,442 = 489,177, rounding to $489.2M).
- **Debt:** $602.8 million of convertible debt on the balance sheet at June 30, 2026 (up from $311.7 million at Dec 31, 2025), reflecting the new $525.0 million 0.5% convertible notes due 2032, net of the repurchase of ~$221 million face value of the 2.25% convertible notes due 2029. Scheduled near-term cash maturities of the remaining 2029 notes are not broken out in the sections reviewed; see the Convertible Debt note (R16/R60/R61) in the 10-Q for the full amortization schedule, which was not separately extracted in this review.
- **Post-quarter cash use (subsequent event):** In July 2026 the company made a $112.5 million upfront cash payment to Everest Medicines for the civorebrutinib license, reducing pro forma cash from the reported $489.2 million quarter-end balance. Up to ~$1.03 billion of additional milestone payments to Everest could be owed over time, contingent on clinical/regulatory/commercial success across up to five indications — this is a real future funding need but not a near-term, dated obligation.
- **Management guidance on runway/going concern:** Not disclosed in filings reviewed — no explicit management statement on "quarters of cash runway" was found in the 10-Q or 8-Ks reviewed, consistent with Travere no longer framing itself around a burn-based runway given its commercial revenue base and recent return to quarterly operating cash generation.
- **Three-basis calculation per skill (adapted for a near-breakeven commercial company):** Given positive 1H 2026 operating cash flow, a classical "divide cash by quarterly burn" calculation is not meaningful (it would imply infinite runway on the current trend) and is therefore not shown as a single number; instead, the relevant facts are: (a) the company generated $17.7 million of operating cash in six months on $296.8 million of revenue, consistent with approaching sustained profitability on a product basis before one-time items (R&D/SG&A investment in FSGS launch and HARMONY trial, plus the $40.0 million one-time inducement expense from debt repurchase, are the main drags on GAAP net income); (b) the single largest near-term cash outflow was the $112.5 million civorebrutinib upfront payment in July 2026, a known, dated, contracted amount; (c) no minimum cash covenant was identified in the sections reviewed.
- **Dilution:** Common shares outstanding grew from 90,922,868 (Dec 31, 2025) to 94,174,767 (June 30, 2026), a ~3.6% increase, mainly from stock option exercises ($32.6 million proceeds in 1H 2026) and the ESPP; the 2032 and remaining 2029 convertible notes carry conversion features that could cause further dilution if converted (specific conversion prices/share caps were not extracted from the Convertible Debt note in this review — flagged as not fully verified). No ATM or shelf-offering activity was identified in the 8-Ks reviewed in this window.

**Funding sources not counted in any cash balance above:** Milestone and royalty payments potentially receivable from Chugai Pharmaceutical (Japan sparsentan partner) are a future cash inflow but are not contracted/dated in the filings reviewed, so they are not counted as "available cash" — mentioned as upside only.
