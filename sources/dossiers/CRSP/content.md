## 1. Profile

**Company:** CRISPR Therapeutics AG
**Ticker:** CRSP (Nasdaq Global Market)
**HQ:** Baarerstrasse 14, 6300 Zug, Switzerland (incorporated in Switzerland; R&D operations in Boston, MA and San Francisco, CA; wholly-owned U.S. subsidiary CRISPR Therapeutics, Inc.)
**CIK:** 0001674416

**Filer type:** Domestic filer — files Form 10-Q (quarterly) and Form 10-K (annual), NOT Form 20-F. Verified directly from the SEC EDGAR submissions JSON (`https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001674416.json`): the `filings.recent.form` array shows periodic filings of type "10-Q" (e.g., filed 2026-08-03, 2026-05-04) and "10-K" (e.g., filed 2026-02-12), alongside Forms 3/4/144 and 8-K — all domestic-filer forms. No Form 20-F, 6-K, or 40-F appears anywhere in the filing history reviewed. Although incorporated in Switzerland, CRISPR Therapeutics AG is treated as a U.S. domestic filer for SEC reporting purposes (large majority of trading/shareholder base is U.S.; it does not claim foreign private issuer status).

**Stage classification:**
- **Approved/commercial:** CASGEVY® (exagamglogene autotemcel / exa-cel) — the world's first approved CRISPR/Cas9-based gene-edited cell therapy, for sickle cell disease (SCD) and transfusion-dependent beta thalassemia (TDT), approved in 39 countries across North America, Europe and the Middle East as of the Q2 2026 update (8-K filed 2026-08-03, Ex-99.1). Commercialized by partner Vertex Pharmaceuticals under a collaboration (CRISPR shares economics; Vertex manufactures, leads commercialization).
- **Clinical/preclinical pipeline:** All other programs (in vivo liver editing: CTX310, CTX340, CTX321; CTX460 via SyNTase platform; siRNA CTX611 via Sirius Therapeutics collaboration; CAR-T zugocabtagene geleucel [zugo-cel, formerly CTX112] in autoimmune disease and immuno-oncology; in vivo CAR-T preclinical programs; regenerative medicine CTX213) are development-stage, not yet approved, and not yet generating product revenue for CRISPR.

**Lead program one-liner:** CASGEVY is CRISPR's sole approved/commercial product (via the Vertex collaboration); the Company's next most advanced proprietary clinical assets are zugocabtagene geleucel (zugo-cel, allogeneic CD19 CAR-T) in multiple Phase 1 autoimmune disease trials and a Phase 1/2 B-cell malignancy trial, and CTX310 (ANGPTL3 in vivo liver editing) in a Phase 1b trial for severe hypertriglyceridemia/refractory hypercholesterolemia.

Source: 10-Q for Q2 FY2026 (filed 2026-08-03, accession 0001193125-26-330672), cover page and Item 1 Business/MD&A sections; 8-K Ex-99.1 (filed 2026-08-03, accession 0001193125-26-330644); SEC submissions JSON.

## 2. One-minute read

1. **CASGEVY revenue scaling fast but off a small base:** CASGEVY generated $76 million of (Vertex-reported) net product revenue in Q2 2026, up 78% quarter-over-quarter and 151% year-over-year. Source: 8-K Ex-99.1, filed 2026-08-03 ("Recent Highlights and Outlook" section). Note: this $76 million figure is Vertex's product revenue; CRISPR's own GAAP income statement does not report a corresponding "CASGEVY revenue" line — CRISPR's economics flow through "Collaboration expense, net" (see Section 5/7 notes).
2. **Large, growing cash balance after a debt raise:** Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $2,364.4 million as of June 30, 2026, up from $1,975.8 million at December 31, 2025, driven primarily by $585.4 million of net proceeds from a March 2026 $600.0 million Convertible Senior Notes offering. Source: 10-Q, Liquidity and Capital Resources section (p. "Liquidity and Capital Resources," filed 2026-08-03) and 8-K Ex-99.1.
3. **Net loss narrowed sharply year-over-year:** Q2 2026 net loss was $91.2 million vs. $208.5 million in Q2 2025, largely because Q2 2025 included a $96.3 million acquired in-process R&D charge tied to the Sirius Therapeutics collaboration that did not repeat in Q2 2026 ($2.5 million). Source: 10-Q Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations and 8-K Ex-99.1.
4. **Management guides to ≥24 months of runway as of the 10-Q filing date, without counting Vertex proceeds:** "We expect our existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities will enable us to fund our operating expenses and capital expenditures for at least the next 24 months without giving effect to any additional proceeds we may receive under our license agreements and collaborations, including with Vertex, and any other capital raising transactions we may complete." Source: 10-Q, "Outlook" subsection of Liquidity and Capital Resources, filed 2026-08-03.
5. **Pipeline broadening in 2026 with multiple new Phase 1 starts:** In Q2 2026 the Company initiated Phase 1 trials for CTX340 (refractory hypertension, AGT target) and CTX460 (alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, first SyNTase-platform candidate), and initiated a third zugo-cel Phase 1 basket trial in autoimmune neurologic disease. Source: 8-K Ex-99.1, filed 2026-08-03.

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim quote):**
> "Based on our research and development plans and our timing expectations related to the progress of our programs, we expect our existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities will enable us to fund our operating expenses and capital expenditures for at least the next 24 months without giving effect to any additional proceeds we may receive under our license agreements and collaborations, including with Vertex, and any other capital raising transactions we may complete."
— Source: 10-Q, "Outlook" (within Liquidity and Capital Resources), filed 2026-08-03, accession 0001193125-26-330672. (This guidance, given as of the 10-Q filing date of August 3, 2026, implies funding into at least August 2028.)

**Calculated A — operating burn basis:**
- YTD (six months ended June 30, 2026) net cash used in operating activities = $192,395 thousand (10-Q Statement of Cash Flows)
- Number of quarters in YTD period = 2
- Average quarterly operating cash outflow = $192,395 / 2 = **$96,197.5 thousand/quarter** (~$96.2 million/quarter)
- Cash + investments balance as of June 30, 2026 = $2,364,352 thousand ($291,337 cash and cash equivalents + $2,073,015 marketable securities, per balance sheet; this ties to the $2,364.4 million figure cited in the MD&A/press release)
- Quarters of runway = $2,364,352 / $96,197.5 = **24.58 quarters** (≈ 6.1 years)
- End-date estimate: from June 30, 2026 + ~24.6 quarters (~6.15 years) ≈ **around Q4 2032**

**Calculated B — adding scheduled debt/lease payments:**
- The $600.0 million Convertible Senior Notes due 2031 (issued March 2026) carry a coupon effectively increased to 1.7308% (to offset anticipated Swiss withholding tax), paid semiannually in arrears on March 1 and September 1, beginning September 1, 2026. Annual cash interest = $600,000 thousand × 1.7308% ≈ **$10,385 thousand/year** (≈ $2,596 thousand/quarter). No principal repayment is due before maturity (March 1, 2031) absent early conversion/redemption/repurchase events. (10-Q Note 8 / Liquidity section, filed 2026-08-03.)
- Operating lease liabilities (current + non-current) total $18,646 + $178,904 = $197,550 thousand as of June 30, 2026 (10-Q balance sheet); these payments are already embedded in the operating cash flow figure used in Calculated A (lease payments run through operating activities in CRISPR's cash flow statement; no separate financing cash outflow for leases is broken out in the filing), so they are not added again here to avoid double-counting.
- Adjusted average quarterly outflow (operating burn + Notes interest) = $96,197.5 + $2,596 = **$98,793.5 thousand/quarter**
- Quarters of runway = $2,364,352 / $98,793.5 = **23.93 quarters** (≈ 6.0 years) → end-date estimate ≈ **around Q2 2032**

Both Calculated A and Calculated B substantially exceed management's own stated "at least 24 months" (8 quarters) guidance — the GAAP operating-burn-only calculation is materially more conservative in management's own framing because it does not strip out non-recurring/lumpy items (e.g., Q1 2026 included a $2.5 million IPR&D purchase and one-time Notes issuance costs embedded in financing, not operating, cash flow) and does not reflect management's internal, more granular forward budget assumptions (which are not disclosed). The arithmetic above is a mechanical trailing-run-rate calculation, not a substitute for management's forward-looking statement, and is presented so it can be checked independently.

**Pro forma adjustment for post-balance-sheet financing (from 8-Ks in window):**
- No 8-K filed between the 10-Q filing date (2026-08-03) and today (2026-10-26) discloses a new financing transaction (equity raise, debt issuance, or ATM activity) that would change the cash balance used above. The 8-K filed 2026-10-19 (Items 5.02, 9.01) relates solely to a CFO leadership transition (new CFO Andrew J. Silver; departure of Dr. Raju Prasad) and discloses no capital-raising activity. The 8-K filed 2026-06-04 (Items 5.02, 5.03, 5.07, 9.01) relates solely to 2026 Annual General Meeting matters (approval of the 2026 Stock Option and Incentive Plan, bylaw amendments, and voting results) and also discloses no capital-raising activity.
- **No pro forma adjustment to the runway calculation is warranted** based on the 8-Ks reviewed in this window; the June 30, 2026 cash + investments balance used above remains the most current disclosed figure.

**Funding sources NOT counted in the runway math above:**
- CRISPR's ongoing share of CASGEVY commercial profits from Vertex beyond amounts already reflected in the historical "Collaboration expense, net" line (future growth in CASGEVY sales, e.g., following FDA pediatric approval, is not projected or added to the cash balance).
- Potential future milestone payments from Vertex: the 10-Q discloses that CRISPR "could be entitled to receive from Vertex certain milestone payments aggregating to high eight digits" contingent on research outcomes and regulatory approval in certain areas (10-Q Note 6/7, filed 2026-08-03) — not included in the cash balance as these are contingent/uncollected.
- Up to $557.2 million of remaining capacity under the Company's 2025 At-The-Market (ATM) equity program as of June 30, 2026 (10-Q, Liquidity and Capital Resources, filed 2026-08-03) — not drawn upon or counted as cash on hand.
- Potential proceeds from the Sirius Therapeutics siRNA collaboration (e.g., future option exercises for additional siRNA targets) — amounts not quantified in filings reviewed.
- Grant revenue (a small, non-dilutive but modest revenue source: $0.6 million for 6 months ended June 30, 2026) is already included within operating cash flow and not separately added, but is noted here as a funding source whose future continuation is not guaranteed or sized in the filings.
