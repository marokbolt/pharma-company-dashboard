# CorMedix Inc. (CRMD) — Tearsheet Source Data

## 1. Profile

- **Company:** CorMedix Inc. (d/b/a "CorMedix Therapeutics")
- **Ticker:** Nasdaq Global Market: CRMD
- **CIK:** 0001410098
- **HQ:** 389 Interpace Parkway, Suite 450, Parsippany, NJ 07054
- **Stage classification:** **Commercial-stage.** CorMedix is a profitable, revenue-generating specialty biopharmaceutical company. Its flagship product DefenCath® (taurolidine and heparin) has been commercially launched since 2024 (both hospital inpatient and outpatient hemodialysis settings), and in August 2025 the company closed its acquisition of Melinta Therapeutics, LLC, adding six additional marketed hospital/clinic-focused infectious disease products (REZZAYO®, MINOCIN® for Injection, VABOMERE®, KIMYRSA®, ORBACTIV®, BAXDELA®) plus cardiovascular product TOPROL-XL®. The company is now GAAP profitable (net income of $64.6M for 6M 2026) — this is not an early/mid-stage clinical company in the traditional cash-burn sense, though it does retain an active clinical pipeline (REZZAYO life-cycle expansion, DefenCath pediatric/TPN studies).
- **Lead program one-liner:** DefenCath (taurolidine/heparin catheter lock solution) is the core commercial driver ($66.1M net revenue in Q2 2026 standalone per the earnings release breakdown, within $94.3M total product sales including Melinta); REZZAYO (rezafungin) life-cycle expansion into IFD prophylaxis post-HSCT is the most significant near-term regulatory/clinical catalyst, with an FDA-accepted sNDA under Priority Review (PDUFA target action date Q1 2027).

## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **Q2 2026 total revenue and grant income was $101.9 million, up 157% YoY** from $39.7 million in Q2 2025 — driven by continued DefenCath demand growth plus the full-quarter inclusion of the Melinta portfolio (acquired August 2025, so not in the Q2 2025 comparison). Net income was $26.0 million ($0.33/$0.29 basic/diluted EPS); Adjusted EBITDA was $58.7 million.
2. **Material reimbursement headwind flagged for 2H 2026:** DefenCath's Medicare transitional drug add-on payment (TDAPA) status ended and transitioned to a post-TDAPA "Add-On Adjustment" on July 1, 2026, which CMS calculates at a materially lower reimbursement rate — management explicitly expects a corresponding reduction in DefenCath net pricing/net sales in the second half of 2026 relative to historical periods (partially offset by an expected step-up in the 2027 payment rate).
3. **REZZAYO life-cycle expansion de-risked by two consecutive positive catalysts:** Phase III ReSPECT topline results (announced April 27, 2026) met its primary endpoint — 60.7% fungal-free survival at Day 90 for rezafungin vs. 59.0% for standard antimicrobial regimen (non-inferiority) — and the FDA subsequently accepted the sNDA for Priority Review (announced October 19, 2026) with a PDUFA target action date in Q1 2027; targeted commercial launch in this new indication is 2H 2027 if approved. CorMedix estimates the U.S. market opportunity for this prophylaxis indication at over $2 billion (company estimate).
4. **Full-year 2026 guidance maintained/raised:** management reiterated FY2026 consolidated revenue guidance of $325–$345 million, raised FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $125–$140 million (from an unspecified prior range), and narrowed cash OpEx guidance to $145–$155 million.
5. **Balance sheet and liquidity strengthened:** cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were $256.7 million as of June 30, 2026 (vs. $148.5 million at December 31, 2025, excluding restricted cash), with additional capacity of $22.1 million available under the company's at-the-market (ATM) facility and $15.0 million remaining under its shelf registration. The company also won a Federal Circuit patent-infringement appeal protecting MINOCIN® (June 8, 2026) and dismissed its auditor CBIZ CPAs in favor of Ernst & Young (effective late June/July 2026), following disclosure of a material weakness in internal controls (review of significant, non-routine transactions) reported in the FY2025 Form 10-K.

## 4. Clinical Trial Results Summary

**Most significant readout in window: Phase III ReSPECT trial (REZZAYO / rezafungin), topline results announced April 27, 2026 (8-K Item 7.01/9.01, Exhibit 99.1).**
- Population: Adult patients undergoing allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT), a high-risk, immunosuppressed population.
- Design: Global, randomized, double-blind, multicenter Phase III study — one of the largest antifungal prophylaxis trials of its kind; rezafungin (once weekly) vs. standard antimicrobial regimen (SAR) — controlled, blinded.
- Primary endpoint: Fungal-free survival at Day 90, assessed for both FDA and EMA requirements. Result: non-inferiority met — 60.7% fungal-free survival (rezafungin) vs. 59.0% (SAR).
- Secondary endpoints: Comparable efficacy against invasive infections caused by Candida, Aspergillus, and Pneumocystis in both arms; comparable mortality between arms; favorable differentiation for rezafungin on treatment-emergent adverse events leading to dose reduction/interruption/withdrawal, and treatment-emergent adverse events leading to study discontinuation (i.e., better tolerability/fewer drug-drug-interaction-related discontinuations).
- Safety: Rezafungin was well tolerated, with a safety profile described as comparable to / favorable versus the standard antimicrobial regimen.
- Data cut-off date: Not explicitly disclosed in the press release; results characterized as "topline" (preliminary) as of the April 27, 2026 announcement.
- Regulatory follow-through: Sponsor (Mundipharma, ex-U.S. rights holder and development sponsor) and Melinta/CorMedix (U.S. distribution rights) pursued a pre-NDA meeting with FDA, followed by sNDA submission; FDA accepted the sNDA for Priority Review on October 19, 2026 (PDUFA target action date Q1 2027) — confirming the April trial data was sufficient to support a regulatory filing.
- No other new clinical trial efficacy/safety readouts for DefenCath, MINOCIN, VABOMERE, KIMYRSA/ORBACTIV, BAXDELA, or TOPROL-XL were disclosed within the 8-K/10-Q window reviewed (April 1 – October 19, 2026); these products' regulatory/clinical history (their original approvals) predates this review window and is not disclosed in filings reviewed for this report.

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, from 10-Q "Funding Requirements and Liquidity"):**
> "We currently estimate that as of June 30, 2026, we have sufficient cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments to fund operations for at least twelve months from the issuance of these financial statements."

(Nearly identical language also appears in the Q2 2026 earnings press release: "The Company believes that it has sufficient resources to fund operations for at least twelve months from the issuance of the Company's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q.")

**Important framing: CorMedix is GAAP-profitable and cash-flow-positive, not a pre-revenue burn-rate company.** Traditional "runway" math (cash ÷ quarterly burn) is less applicable here than for a clinical-stage biotech, because the company generated $128.6 million of positive operating cash flow in 6M 2026. The calculations below are provided for completeness per the house framework, but the more relevant risk factor is reimbursement-driven revenue compression (post-TDAPA DefenCath pricing), not a cash-runway shortfall.

**Calculated A — operating basis:** With cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $256.7 million as of June 30, 2026 and positive operating cash flow of $128.6 million for the trailing six months, the company's basis is cash-generative, not cash-consuming. No burn-rate-based runway exhaustion date is calculable because net operating cash flow is positive. The key forward risk is the magnitude of the post-TDAPA reimbursement step-down on DefenCath net pricing in 2H 2026 (quantified range not disclosed in filings reviewed) and the previously discussed $9.0 million one-time negative change-in-estimate for Medicaid/returns sales allowances recorded in 6M 2026.

**Calculated B — including debt service:** CorMedix carries $145.15 million of convertible senior notes (net of deferred financing costs) as a long-term liability as of June 30, 2026, plus total contingent consideration liabilities (short + long-term) of $111.6 million related to the Melinta acquisition earn-out. Cash paid for interest in 6M 2026 was only $2.8 million (low cash interest burden consistent with a convertible note structure), so near-term debt service is not a material drain on liquidity. No near-term maturity date for the convertible notes was disclosed in the sections of the 10-Q reviewed; not disclosed in filings reviewed.

**Pro forma (post-balance-sheet-date items):** The 10-Q discloses that the company intends to establish a letter of credit in Q3 2026 totaling approximately $59 million in connection with new customer payment-term arrangements, which will require an equal amount of cash to be classified as restricted cash on the balance sheet. Management states this is not expected to impair its ability to operate as a going concern, but it will reduce unrestricted cash by roughly $59 million once established (pro forma unrestricted cash of approximately $256.7M − $59M ≈ $198M, all else equal) — a disclosed, quantified near-term use of cash not yet reflected in the June 30, 2026 balance sheet.

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- $22.1 million remaining availability under the at-the-market (ATM) common stock issuance sales agreement, as of June 30, 2026.
- $15.0 million remaining available under the company's shelf registration statement, as of June 30, 2026.
- Ongoing positive operating cash generation itself (not a "reserve" but a continuing funding source not embedded in the static cash balance).
- Potential future REZZAYO milestone/royalty economics tied to Priority Review approval and 2H 2027 commercial launch (amounts not disclosed in filings reviewed).
- No warrants or stock options outstanding balance was disclosed in the specific sections reviewed; not disclosed in filings reviewed.
