# Tango Therapeutics, Inc. (TNGX) — Tearsheet Source Data

**Window used:** Mar 1, 2026 – Oct 26, 2026 (anchor quarter: Q2 2026, ended Jun 30, 2026). Pre-quarter lookback starts ~30 days before Apr 1, 2026; subsequent-events coverage runs from the 10-Q filing date (Aug 11, 2026) through today (Oct 26, 2026), which captures one clearly quarter-relevant follow-up 8-K (Oct 23, 2026, updated PDAC combination data).

## 1. Profile

- **Company:** Tango Therapeutics, Inc. | **Ticker:** TNGX (Nasdaq) | **CIK:** 0001819133
- **HQ:** Boston, Massachusetts
- **Stage classification:** **Mid stage** — lead program vopimetostat (TNG462) is in Phase 1/2 combination trials; the company has announced plans to start a registrational Phase 3 trial in 1H 2027, but no Phase 3 trial has been initiated as of the 10-Q or the Oct 23, 2026 8-K.
- **Lead program one-liner:** Vopimetostat (TNG462), an oral PRMT5 inhibitor, is being developed for MTAP-deleted cancers, both as a monotherapy and — the company's most important near-term catalyst — in combination with Revolution Medicines' RAS(ON) inhibitors (daraxonrasib and zoldonrasib) in MTAP-deleted, RAS-mutant pancreatic cancer (PDAC).

## 2. One-minute read

- **Cash position surged to ~$1.0 billion** (cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities: $345.6M + $668.4M = $1,013,990 thousand) at June 30, 2026, up from $343.1M at Dec 31, 2025, after a June 2026 underwritten offering of common stock and pre-funded warrants that raised $651.4 million net (10-Q, Liquidity and Capital Resources).
- **Updated PDAC combination data (Oct 23, 2026, 8-K Ex-99.1, post-10-Q):** vopimetostat + daraxonrasib showed an 82% ORR (18/22 efficacy-evaluable patients), median PFS not yet reached, and 88% 9-month PFS rate in previously treated MTAP-deleted/RAS-mutant PDAC (data cut-off Aug 20, 2026) — materially larger and longer-follow-up than the initial June 2026 dataset (92% ORR in 12 patients) cited in the 10-Q.
- **Registrational Phase 3 trial of vopimetostat + daraxonrasib in front-line MTAP-deleted PDAC planned for 1H 2027**, a randomized 1:1 trial vs. standard-of-care chemotherapy with dual primary endpoints of ORR and PFS (Oct 23, 2026 8-K).
- **Pipeline rationalization:** the company deprioritized/is shutting down the Phase 1/2 TNG260 trial (May 2026) and will not further advance TNG961 (May 2026), sharpening focus on the two PRMT5 assets, vopimetostat and TNG456 (brain-penetrant, for GBM).
- **G&A expenses nearly doubled** to $22.6 million in Q2 2026 from $11.3 million in Q2 2025, while net loss widened to $55.3 million (Q2 2026) from $38.9 million (Q2 2025) (10-Q income statement) — driven in part by the wind-down of Gilead collaboration revenue (zero in 2026 vs. $3.2M/$8.6M recognized in Q2/H1 2025).

## 6. Runway calculations

- **Management's own guidance (verbatim):** "We expect that our existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities on hand as of June 30, 2026 of $1.0 billion will enable us to meet our current operating plan and fund our operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements for at least twelve months from the issuance of the financial statements in this Quarterly Report." (10-Q, MD&A, Funding Requirements).
- **Calculated A (operating burn basis):** H1 2026 net cash used in operating activities = $83.226M over 2 quarters = ~$41.6M/quarter average quarterly operating burn. Cash + marketable securities at Jun 30, 2026 = $345,558 + $668,432 = $1,013,990 thousand (≈$1.01B, consistent with MD&A's "$1.0 billion"). $1,013,990 ÷ $41.6M/quarter ≈ **24.4 quarters (≈6.1 years), i.e., about Q4 2032.**
- **Calculated B (adjusted burn, debt/lease):** No outstanding debt is disclosed on the balance sheet. Operating lease liabilities total $2.9M (current) + $29.0M (non-current) = $31.99M, a contractual obligation already embedded in operating cash flow via lease payments; no separate debt amortization schedule is disclosed in filings reviewed, so Calculated B = Calculated A ("not disclosed in filings reviewed" for a distinct debt repayment schedule).
- **Pro forma:** No financing event closed after the June 30, 2026 balance-sheet date was identified in 8-Ks filed through Oct 26, 2026 (the one subsequent 8-K, Oct 23, 2026, is clinical-data only, no financing); so pro forma = Calculated A.
- **Sensitivity note:** Calculated A's implied quarterly operating cash burn (~$41.6M) is noticeably lower than Q2 2026's standalone quarterly net loss ($55.3M) or total operating expenses ($59.8M), reflecting non-cash items (stock-based compensation, etc.) not broken out separately in the excerpted cash flow statement; this difference exceeds the skill's 20% sensitivity threshold and should be flagged as a gap between P&L run-rate and operating cash burn.
- **Note on the large disparity between management's "at least 12 months" guidance and the multi-year Calculated A:** management's 12-month statement is the standard conservative US GAAP going-concern assertion, not a forecast of when cash is exhausted; the $1.0B cash balance built from the June 2026 raise supports a materially longer calculated runway than the mandatory minimum disclosure.
- **Funding sources not counted in the runway math:**
  - Remaining capacity under the November 2025 Leerink Sales Agreement (ATM): $100.0M aggregate authorized, $64.4M gross sold through Jun 30, 2026, leaving approximately $35.6 million in remaining ATM capacity as of the 10-Q filing date.
  - Pre-funded warrants issued in the June 2026 offering (1,833,395 warrants, exercise price $0.001/share) are immediately exercisable; proceeds from exercise (de minimis, near-$0.001 incremental cash per share) are not material new cash and are not separately counted.
  - No contracted, dated partner milestones are disclosed as outstanding following the conclusion of the Gilead collaboration in August 2025; "not disclosed in filings reviewed" for any other partner milestone stream.
