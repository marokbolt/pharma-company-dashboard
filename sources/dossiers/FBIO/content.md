# Fortress Biotech, Inc. (FBIO) — Tearsheet Source Data

## 1. Profile

- **Company:** Fortress Biotech, Inc.
- **Ticker:** Nasdaq: FBIO (common); Nasdaq: FBIOP (9.375% Series A Cumulative Redeemable Perpetual Preferred Stock)
- **CIK:** 0001429260
- **HQ:** 1111 Kane Concourse, Suite 301, Bay Harbor Islands, FL 33154
- **Business model:** Holding company / biopharmaceutical "incubator" model. Fortress identifies and acquires/licenses drug candidates, then develops them at majority-owned/controlled subsidiaries (consolidated) and minority-owned partner companies (equity-method/fair-value, not consolidated). Value accrues to the parent via product revenue (consolidated subsidiary Journey Medical), royalties, milestones, dividends and equity stakes in partner companies (e.g., Crystalys, Checkpoint/Sun Pharma, AstraZeneca-owned Caelum program).
- **Stage classification:** Fortress itself is best classified as a **Commercial-stage / diversified holding company** — it has one wholly/majority-controlled commercial subsidiary (Journey Medical, marketed dermatology products) generating real net product revenue (~$17.8M in Q2 2026), plus royalty-stage products (ZYCUBO, UNLOXCYT), a late-stage partnered asset (dotinurad/Crystalys, Phase 3), a late-stage out-licensed program with regulatory submissions pending (anselamimab/CAEL-101 at AstraZeneca), and multiple early/mid-stage subsidiary pipeline programs (Triplex–Phase 2, MB-109, ATX-04, FB-606 — preclinical/early clinical). Because of this spread, program-level stage varies widely; see pipeline table below for each asset's own stage.
- **Lead program (one-liner):** Journey Medical's Emrosi® (minocycline HCl modified-release, DFD-29) drives current consolidated revenue; Crystalys's dotinurad (Phase 3, gout) and AstraZeneca's anselamimab/CAEL-101 (AL amyloidosis, regulatory submissions pending in EU/Japan) are the two most value-relevant partnered late-stage programs for equity holders.

**Important structural note:** Fortress consolidates Journey Medical, Mustang Bio, Avenue Therapeutics, Cellvation, Cyprium, Helocyte, LemmaTx, Oncogenuity and Urica Therapeutics (majority-owned/controlled). It does **not** consolidate Crystalys Therapeutics (minority equity stake held via Urica, ~10% post-Series B) or AstraZeneca (owns the CAEL-101/anselamimab program outright after acquiring Caelum in 2021; Fortress receives milestone/royalty economics only). All consolidated financial statements below are Fortress Biotech, Inc. AND SUBSIDIARIES (GAAP consolidated, including non-controlling interests of partly-owned but controlled subsidiaries such as Journey, Mustang, Avenue). Figures for Crystalys, Checkpoint/Sun Pharma, and AstraZeneca's anselamimab program are **not** part of Fortress's consolidated financials — they appear only as equity investment fair-value marks, royalty/contingent-consideration income, or milestone eligibility.

## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **Cash jumped to $196.6M (+$117.2M YTD)** as of June 30, 2026, driven almost entirely by the $205M sale of the ZYCUBO priority review voucher (PRV) in Q1 2026 (gain on sale, net of expenses: $158.9M for the six months). This is a one-time, non-recurring cash inflow, not a sign of a sustainably improving operating cash burn.
2. **Consolidated net revenue grew 14% YoY to $18.7M in Q2 2026** (vs. $16.4M in Q2 2025), driven by Journey Medical's dermatology portfolio (Emrosi net product revenue $17.8M, up from $15.0M), now with payer access covering ~85% of commercial lives after a third GPO contract.
3. **Debt materially de-risked post-quarter-end:** the $50M Oaktree senior secured credit facility (which stood at $15.0M outstanding as of June 30, 2026) was fully repaid on September 30, 2026 (8-K, Item 8.01), eliminating the Company's remaining funded debt and associated covenants.
4. **Key late-stage partnered readout was a mixed/negative headline but with a notable subgroup signal:** AstraZeneca's Phase 3 CARES program for anselamimab (CAEL-101, AL amyloidosis) missed its primary endpoint (disclosed July 16, 2025, reiterated in Q2 2026 release) but showed a 62% survival improvement and 71% reduction in cardiovascular hospitalizations in a prespecified kappa-predominant subgroup; AstraZeneca plans to submit this subgroup analysis to regulators (submissions already under review in EU and Japan for the broader program).
5. **Consolidated net income attributable to common stockholders swung to a loss of $(2.5)M, or $(0.08)/share, in Q2 2026**, versus net income of $13.4M ($0.50/share basic) in Q2 2025 — the prior-year quarter benefited from a one-time $27.1M gain on deconsolidation of a subsidiary; excluding one-time items, underlying operating loss narrowed meaningfully (loss from operations improved to $(10.0)M from $(36.5)M YoY on lower R&D and SG&A).

## 4. Clinical Trial Results Summary

- **Anselamimab (CAEL-101) — Phase 3 CARES program, prespecified subgroup analysis.** Disclosed in Q2 2026 (per Aug 13, 2026 8-K/press release), updating the July 16, 2025 primary readout.
  - Population: Kappa-predominant light chain isotype patients with AL amyloidosis (subgroup of the full CARES Mayo stage IIIa/IIIb population).
  - Design: Two global Phase 3 pivotal, randomized trials (NCT04512235, NCT04504825), anselamimab + standard of care vs. placebo + standard of care (controlled; blinding not specified in excerpt reviewed).
  - Endpoints/results reported for subgroup: 62% improvement in survival (time to all-cause mortality; HR 0.38, 95% CI 0.17–0.86, nominal p=0.012); 71% reduction in cardiovascular hospitalizations (incidence risk ratio 0.29, 95% CI 0.10–0.87, nominal p=0.028), both vs. placebo.
  - Primary endpoint (full population, originally disclosed July 16, 2025): anselamimab did NOT achieve statistical significance on the hierarchical combination of time-to-all-cause-mortality and cardiovascular hospitalization frequency, vs. placebo. Drug reported as well tolerated with balanced adverse events between arms.
  - Data cut-off date: not disclosed in filings reviewed (AstraZeneca-run trial; Fortress's filings report headline results only, not full study report dates).
  - Regulatory status: EU and Japan regulatory applications already under review; AstraZeneca plans to submit the new subgroup analysis to additional regulatory authorities.
- **Dotinurad — no new efficacy/safety readout in this window** beyond enrollment/initiation milestones (Phase 2 AMETHYST first patients dosed May 2026; two Phase 3 trials ongoing with no interim data disclosed). Most recent dotinurad clinical operational milestone: first patients dosed in Phase 2 AMETHYST trial, announced May 2026.
- **MB-101 (IL13Rα2 CAR T)** — most recent trial data disclosed is from the Phase 1 trial published in Nature Medicine in March 2024 (outside this 10-Q's window; included here per instructions since no newer MB-101 clinical data appeared in the reviewed window): well tolerated; 50% of patients achieved stable disease or better (two partial responses, two complete responses) in high-grade glioma; complete responders had high intratumoral CD3+ T-cell levels pre-therapy; durability of complete responses 7.5 and 66+ months; dual intratumoral/intraventricular delivery cohort showed ~70% improvement in median overall survival (10.2 months) vs. ~6-month expected survival in this population. Not a controlled/randomized trial — single-arm Phase 1.
- **Triplex HIV/CMV co-infection Phase 2 trial** — no efficacy data disclosed yet in this window; trial is fully enrolled with topline data anticipated late 2026 (not yet available as of the 10-Q filing date, Aug 13, 2026).

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, from 10-Q Note on Liquidity and Capital Resources):**
> "Current cash and cash equivalents as of June 30, 2026 of $153.8 million for Fortress and private subsidiaries primarily funded by Fortress ('Parent Entity') are considered sufficient to fund the Parent Entity's operations for at least 12 months following the date of filing of this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q."

Note: this guidance explicitly covers only the **"Fortress and private subsidiaries"** cash pool ($153.8M of the $196.6M consolidated total as of 6/30/26) — it excludes cash held at Avenue ($1.9M), Mustang Bio ($15.1M) and Journey Medical ($25.6M), which are separately funded, partly public entities with their own disclosures.

**Calculated A — operating burn basis (consolidated, GAAP operating cash flow):**
- Six-month 2026 operating cash flow was **positive** ($144.6M), but this is almost entirely attributable to the one-time $205M PRV sale (gain on sale, net of expenses, of $158.9M flowed through net income and the operating section). Excluding this one-time item, underlying operating cash burn for 6M 2026 would be roughly $144.6M − $158.9M (proceeds impact, non-cash gain adjustment aside; actual cash from PRV sale would flow largely through investing/other, but the gain itself is embedded in net income used as CFO starting point) ≈ a burn in the same range as the prior-year comparable period, approximately $(14)M to $(47)M for six months (6M 2025 operating cash used was $(47.1)M for comparison, though cost structure has since been reduced — R&D fell from $12.1M to $1.3M YoY for the six-month period and SG&A fell from $64.4M to $36.7M).
- A cleaner burn proxy: Loss from operations for 6M 2026 was $(17.7)M (vs. $(58.8)M in 6M 2025), implying an annualized consolidated operating loss run-rate of roughly $35M/year at the current (post-cost-reduction) expense structure.
- At consolidated cash of $196.6M (or Parent-entity-only cash of $153.8M) against an annualized operating loss run-rate of ~$35M consolidated, runway on a pure burn basis would exceed 12 months by a wide margin — consistent with, and more conservative than, management's own 12-month assertion for the Parent Entity pool alone.

**Calculated B — including debt service:**
- As of June 30, 2026, Notes payable (current + long-term) totaled $5,000 (short-term, "Partner company notes payable") + $34,602 (long-term, net) = ~$39.6M, substantially the 2024 Oaktree Note ($15.0M principal outstanding at 6/30/26 per the 10-Q narrative, net of discount/issuance costs reflected in the $34.6M long-term carrying value which may include other partner-company debt — not fully itemized by instrument in the excerpt reviewed).
- **Material post-period event:** On September 30, 2026, Fortress fully repaid the remaining $15.0M Oaktree principal plus ~$0.435M accrued interest, a $0.15M prepayment fee and other closing costs (8-K, Item 8.01, filed Oct 1, 2026). This retires the Oaktree facility entirely, removing associated covenants and reducing the "Calculated B" debt-service drag that existed as of the 10-Q's balance-sheet date.

**Pro forma (post-balance-sheet-date financing/repayment):**
- Pro forma for the Oaktree repayment, Fortress's cash position (consolidated $196.6M as of 6/30/26) would be reduced by approximately $15.6M (principal + interest + fees), to roughly $181M consolidated, but with zero remaining funded parent-level debt and no further covenant compliance risk from that facility. No new financing was disclosed as having closed between the 10-Q filing date (Aug 13, 2026) and today (per filings reviewed through Oct 19, 2026, the date of the latest 8-K/exhibit reviewed for this report).

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- Equity investment in Crystalys Therapeutics (carried at fair value, $29.5M as of 6/30/26) — a partial-liquidity, non-cash asset not part of operating cash runway.
- Up to $128 million in aggregate sales milestones and 3%–12.5% tiered royalties on ZYCUBO net sales (Cyprium).
- Up to ~$182 million in aggregate milestone/royalty eligibility from AstraZeneca tied to anselamimab/CAEL-101 regulatory and commercial milestones (42.4% of payments due to former Caelum shareholders).
- Up to $4.8 million CVR milestone from Sun Pharma tied to EU approval of UNLOXCYT.
- 2.5% royalty stream from Sun Pharma on UNLOXCYT net sales (ongoing, not reflected in any runway calculation above).
- 3% royalty on future dotinurad/Crystalys net sales (contingent on Phase 3 success and commercialization — years away).
- Rule 144-permitted ability to sell shares of publicly traded partner companies (Journey Medical, Mustang Bio) it holds as additional non-dilutive liquidity source.
- No ATM facility balance, shelf registration headroom, or warrants/options outstanding figures were disclosed in the specific sections of the 10-Q reviewed for this report; not disclosed in filings reviewed.
