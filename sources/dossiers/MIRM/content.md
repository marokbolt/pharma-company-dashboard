## 1. Profile

Mirum Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is a commercial-stage rare-disease biopharmaceutical company headquartered at 989 East Hillsdale Boulevard, Suite 300, Foster City, CA, incorporated in Delaware, trading on Nasdaq as MIRM. **Stage classification:** Commercial — the company markets three approved rare-liver-disease products (LIVMARLI® (maralixibat) for Alagille syndrome and PFIC; CHOLBAM® (cholic acid) for bile acid synthesis disorders; CTEXLI® (chenodiol) for cerebrotendinous xanthomatosis) generating substantial product revenue, and as of September 25, 2026 also holds a newly FDA-approved fourth product, Atebrioz™ (zilurgisertib) for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP). Its clinical pipeline is led by volixibat (Phase 2b/pre-NDA stage, cholestatic pruritus due to PSC and PBC) and brelovitug (Phase 3, chronic hepatitis delta virus), the latter acquired via the Bluejay Therapeutics acquisition completed January 26, 2026.

## 2. One-minute read

- **Q2 2026 global net product sales were $176.2 million**, up from $127.8 million in Q2 2025 (+38%); management raised full-year 2026 net product sales guidance to **$680–$700 million** from prior guidance (prior figure not disclosed in filings reviewed). LIVMARLI net product sales grew 46% year-over-year to $128.7 million in Q2 2026; Bile Acid Medicines (CHOLBAM/CTEXLI) grew 20% to $47.5 million. *(Source: 8-K Ex-99.1, Aug 5, 2026.)*
- **Net loss was $857.4 million for the six months ended June 30, 2026** (vs. $20.5 million in 1H 2025), driven almost entirely by a one-time, non-cash $742.7 million acquired in-process research and development (IPR&D) charge, of which $726.3 million relates to the January 26, 2026 acquisition of Bluejay Therapeutics (adding brelovitug for HDV) and $16.4 million (in Q2 2026 alone) relates to the exclusive in-license of zilurgisertib from Incyte. Excluding this one-time non-cash IPR&D charge, underlying operating performance improved (Q2 2026 product-sales growth outpaced opex growth before IPR&D). *(Source: 10-Q R4; 8-K Ex-99.1.)*
- **The company refinanced and upsized its convertible debt in Q2 2026**: issued $690.0 million of 0.00% convertible senior notes due 2032 and used proceeds to settle $237.2 million (approximately 75% of the then-outstanding principal) of its 4.00% convertible notes due 2029; convertible notes payable rose from $309.8 million (Dec 31, 2025) to $750.1 million (June 30, 2026). Unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and investments were $561.3 million at June 30, 2026, up from $391.4 million at Dec 31, 2025. *(Source: 10-Q R2, R8; 8-K Ex-99.1.)*
- **Two FDA regulatory wins occurred in the window**: Atebrioz (zilurgisertib) was approved September 25, 2026 for FOP (PDUFA date had been September 26, 2026), becoming the company's fourth approved product; separately, volixibat received FDA Breakthrough Therapy and Orphan Drug designations for cholestatic pruritus due to PSC in the quarter, though at a pre-NDA meeting the FDA recommended an additional Phase 3 study, pushing potential NDA submission to H1 2027 (previously implied to be sooner). *(Source: 8-K Sept 28, 2026; 8-K Ex-99.1 Aug 5, 2026.)*
- **Subsequent to quarter-end, the Phase 3 EXPAND study of LIVMARLI met its primary and all five secondary endpoints** in ultra-rare cholestatic liver diseases including biliary atresia (announced October 12, 2026): a statistically significant 0.98-point placebo-adjusted improvement in pruritus (p=0.0004) and a 69.0 percentage-point placebo-adjusted reduction in serum bile acids (p<0.0001) in a 52-patient randomized, placebo-controlled trial; a supplemental NDA submission is planned for H1 2027. *(Source: 8-K Oct 13, 2026.)*

## 6. Runway calculations

Mirum is a growing commercial company with multiple approved products and positive underlying commercial momentum, but it is **currently burning cash on a GAAP operating cash flow basis** in 1H 2026 due to heavy R&D investment (including the newly acquired brelovitug Phase 3 program) and acquisition-related cash items; it is not a near-breakeven company in the way TVTX is this quarter, so the three-basis burn method below is applied.

- **Management guidance (quoted):** The company states it has "Financial Independence" in the Aug 5, 2026 earnings release headline ("Strong Balance Sheet and Financial Independence") and reports unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $561.3 million at June 30, 2026 vs. $391.4 million at Dec 31, 2025, but **does not give an explicit quarters-of-runway or going-concern statement** in the sections of the filings reviewed — flagged as "not disclosed in filings reviewed."
- **Cash position:** $330.7 million cash and cash equivalents + $107.6 million short-term investments + $123.0 million long-term investments + $1.7 million restricted cash = **$563.0 million** total per the balance sheet (company's own $561.3 million figure in the press release excludes restricted cash, which reconciles: $330,728 + $107,563 + $123,030 = $561,321 ≈ $561.3 million, confirmed).
- **Calculated A, operating burn (per skill method, using year-to-date average):** 1H 2026 operating cash outflow was $273.0 million over 2 quarters = average quarterly burn of **$136.5 million/quarter**. On a cash balance of $561.3 million (excluding restricted cash), this implies **~4.1 quarters (about Q3 2027)** of runway on the raw 1H 2026 average burn rate. This calculation should be read cautiously: the 1H 2026 burn rate is distorted upward by one-time, acquisition-linked cash items (e.g., the $71.5 million net positive swing from holdback liabilities and working-capital items tied to the Bluejay deal) that are unlikely to recur at the same magnitude each quarter.
- **Calculated B, adjusted burn:** No scheduled near-term debt repayment was identified in the sections reviewed (the 2032 convertible notes mature June 1, 2032, and the 2029 notes were mostly settled in May 2026); lease payments are a minor addition not separately broken out in this review. No minimum cash covenant was identified, so no Calculated C.
- **Sensitivity (latest quarter run rate):** Q2 2026 alone cannot be cleanly isolated for operating cash flow from the 6-month cash flow statement as presented (the filing shows year-to-date only, per the skill's standard caveat about 10-Q cash flow statements); a quarter-only operating cash flow figure is therefore **not disclosed in filings reviewed** and was not estimated.
- **Pro forma:** No financing event after the June 30, 2026 balance sheet date was identified in the 8-Ks reviewed through October 23, 2026 (the convertible notes offering and private placement both closed within 1H 2026, before the balance sheet date), so no pro forma adjustment is made.
- **Dilution:** Common shares outstanding grew from 51,896,391 (Dec 31, 2025) to 64,754,699 (June 30, 2026), a ~24.8% increase, driven mainly by the Bluejay acquisition stock consideration ($415.3 million fair value of stock issued, per the cash flow statement) and the private placement that closed concurrently with the acquisition ($259.9 million gross proceeds per the May/Jan 2026 press releases, with net proceeds of $259.948 million shown in the 6mo 2026 cash flow statement). The new 2032 convertible notes (0.00% coupon, $690.0 million principal) carry conversion rights that could cause further dilution; specific conversion price/share-cap terms were not extracted from the Convertible Notes note (R18/R59-61) in this review — flagged as not fully verified.

**Funding sources not counted in any cash balance above:** Potential milestone payments owed to former Bluejay Therapeutics shareholders (structured as "holdback liabilities" of $24.8 million current and $61.2 million non-current on the balance sheet) are already recognized as liabilities, not upside, and are noted here for completeness rather than as runway-extending cash.
