## 1. Profile

IDEAYA Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: IDYA) is a South San Francisco, California-based, Delaware-incorporated, clinical-stage precision medicine oncology company founded in 2015. It develops small-molecule and antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) therapeutics for molecularly defined solid tumors, built around synthetic lethality drug discovery. Its lead, most advanced asset is darovasertib (oral PKC inhibitor) in combination with crizotinib, partnered globally ex-U.S. with Les Laboratoires Servier, in registrational trials for metastatic and adjuvant uveal melanoma (mUM); the company plans to submit a U.S. NDA under the FDA's Real-Time Oncology Review (RTOR) program in H2 2026. Stage classification: **Late stage** on the lead asset (registrational Phase 2/3, NDA filing underway), with a broad mid-to-early-stage pipeline of ADC and synthetic-lethality programs (IDE849, IDE034, IDE892, IDE397, IDE161, IDE574) behind it. No approved products or product revenue as of the window.

## 2. One-minute read

- **Cash position jumped to ~$1.24 billion** as of June 30, 2026 (up from $972.9 million at March 31, 2026 and $1,049.7 million at December 31, 2025), driven mainly by a $323.4 million net-proceeds follow-on equity offering that closed June 10, 2026 and $33.1 million of ATM sales in the quarter (10-Q, p.4; 8-K filed 2026-08-04 Ex-99.1).
- **Lead asset hit its primary endpoint in a registrational trial**: the Phase 2/3 OptimUM-02 trial of darovasertib + crizotinib in first-line HLA*A2:01-negative metastatic uveal melanoma showed median PFS of 6.9 vs. 3.1 months by BICR (HR 0.42, p<0.0001) at a January 23, 2026 data cutoff; NDA submission targeted for H2 2026 under RTOR (8-K filed 2026-06-01 and 2026-04-13, Item 8.01; 10-Q MD&A).
- **Net loss widened to $112.5 million in Q2 2026** (vs. $98.5 million in Q1 2026 and $77.5 million in Q2 2025), driven by R&D expense growth to $108.7 million as clinical spend scales across the ADC and MTAP-pathway programs (10-Q Statements of Operations).
- **Management reaffirmed cash runway guidance "into 2030"** unchanged from prior guidance, based on ~$1.24 billion of cash/investments as of June 30, 2026 (8-K Ex-99.1, filed 2026-08-04); this is materially longer than the statutory 12-month going-concern statement in the 10-Q itself.
- **Window event**: the company completed a ~$345.0 million gross ($323.4 million net) underwritten public offering of common stock and pre-funded warrants in June 2026 (closed June 10, 2026), and elected a new Board Chair (Yujiro Hata) and Lead Independent Director (Terry Rosen) following the June 16, 2026 Annual Meeting (8-Ks filed 2026-06-10, 2026-06-23).

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim):**
- Earnings release (8-K filed Aug 4, 2026, Ex-99.1): *"~$1.24 billion of cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026; cash runway guidance into 2030 is unchanged based on current operating plan."*
- 10-Q going-concern/liquidity statement (statutory 12-month test, narrower than the guidance horizon above): *"Management believes that the Company's current cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities will be sufficient to fund its planned operations for at least twelve months from the date of the issuance of this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q"* (filed August 4, 2026).

**Calculated A — operating burn basis:**
- Six-month (2 quarters) net cash used in operating activities = $163,381K → average quarterly operating burn = $81,690.5K.
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities at June 30, 2026 = $1,242,889K (sum of cash and cash equivalents $272,589K + short-term marketable securities $556,366K + long-term marketable securities $413,934K).
- Quarters of runway = $1,242,889K / $81,690.5K ≈ **15.2 quarters** ≈ 45.6 months from June 30, 2026 → **end point of approximately Q1 2030**, consistent with management's "into 2030" guidance.

**Calculated B — adjusted burn (debt/lease):**
- IDEAYA carries no outstanding debt and only operating leases (no finance leases). Scheduled operating lease payments are already embedded in operating cash flow (the "changes in lease liabilities" line, +$1,393K inflow for the six months, within Calculated A's burn). There is no separate debt-service or finance-lease cash outflow to add, so Calculated B equals Calculated A (**≈15.2 quarters, into Q1 2030**).

**Pro forma:**
- The company's major financing event of the window — the ~$345.0 million gross ($323.4 million net) follow-on offering of common stock and pre-funded warrants — closed June 10, 2026, i.e., *before* the June 30, 2026 balance-sheet date, and is therefore already fully reflected in the cash balance and in Calculated A/B above. No additional post-balance-sheet-date financing had closed between the 10-Q filing date (August 4, 2026) and the as-of date (October 26, 2026) per filings reviewed. No separate pro forma adjustment is needed.

**Latest-quarter run-rate sensitivity:** Quarter-only operating cash flow is not separately disclosed (cash flow statement is six-month, year-to-date only per SEC convention); Q2 2026 net loss ($112.5 million) was about 14% higher than Q1 2026 ($98.5 million), driven by rising R&D spend, suggesting burn may be trending modestly above the six-month average — a risk to the stated 2030 runway if the trend continues, though well within the >20% sensitivity threshold is not clearly breached based on available net-loss proxy data (operating-cash-flow quarter split is "not disclosed in filings reviewed").

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- Jefferies at-the-market (ATM) program: approximately $122.6 million of common stock remained available for sale as of June 30, 2026, under the January 2024 Sales Agreement (capacity refreshed to $156.6 million under a new S-3ASR shelf filed May 5, 2026; $33.1 million sold in Q2 2026 reducing it to $122.6 million by quarter-end).
- Shelf registration: new automatic shelf registration statement on Form S-3ASR (Reg. No. 333-295560) filed and effective May 5, 2026, replacing the prior S-3 shelf that was set to expire June 26, 2026.
- No debt facilities outstanding.
- Warrants: pre-funded warrants to purchase 5,555,576 shares of common stock issued in the June 2026 offering at a $0.0001 exercise price (effectively common-stock equivalents, already included in diluted share counts; not a cash inflow source beyond the nominal exercise price).
- Partner/collaboration milestones and cost reimbursements: remaining Servier research-and-development services performance obligation of $147.0 million (recognized as revenue, and substantially reimbursed in cash, over time as R&D services are completed) is not counted as committed cash in the runway calculation above; CRT/Manchester is owed up to £18.75 million in further development/regulatory milestones from IDEAYA (a cash outflow, not inflow); Biocytogen received a $5.0 million milestone in Q1 2026 (cash outflow).
