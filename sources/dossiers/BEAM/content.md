## 1. Profile

Beam Therapeutics Inc. (NASDAQ: BEAM) is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It is a clinical-stage biotechnology company building a base-editing (precision gene editing) platform spanning ex vivo hematology, in vivo liver-targeted genetic disease programs, and multiple industry partnerships (Eli Lilly, Pfizer, Bristol-Myers Squibb via Orbital, and formerly Apellis, now Biogen). Stage classification: **Late-stage lead program** — risto-cel (formerly BEAM-101) has completed dosing in its Phase 1/2 BEACON trial and the company expects to submit a BLA as early as year-end 2026; BEAM-302 (AATD) is Phase 1/2 moving into a pivotal expansion cohort. Other wholly-owned programs (BEAM-301, BEAM-304, BEAM-103) are earlier Phase 1/1-2. Lead program: risto-cel, an autologous base-edited HSC therapy for severe sickle cell disease, BLA submission expected as early as year-end 2026.

## 2. One-minute read

- Risto-cel (sickle cell disease) BLA submission expected as early as year-end 2026; Phase 1/2 BEACON data (dosing complete, ~50 patients) published in the April 1, 2026 NEJM, showing mean HbF >60% and mean editing of 72.8% by Month 12 (source: 10-Q MD&A).
- BEAM-302 (AATD) pivotal cohort dosing began in July 2026 at the 60 mg dose selected as the optimal biological dose, following a Feb 10, 2026 data cut-off showing 84%/79% reductions in mutant Z-AAT in the 60 mg/75 mg cohorts (28 efficacy-evaluable patients) — company is pursuing an accelerated approval pathway.
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $1,152.9 million at 6/30/2026 (down from $1,245.2 million at 12/31/2025); management guides cash runway into mid-2029, inclusive of an additional $200 million it expects to draw under its Sixth Street credit facility.
- Net loss for the six months ended 6/30/2026 was $217.0 million (vs. $210.5 million prior-year period); operating cash burn was $194.6 million for the six months, up from $180.3 million.
- Window event: in February 2026 Beam entered a new $100M-funded (up to $500M total, delayed-draw) Credit Facility with Sixth Street Lending Partners secured against substantially all assets, tied to risto-cel regulatory/commercial milestones, carrying an effective interest rate of 10.5% as of 6/30/2026.

## 4. Clinical trial results summary

Most recent disclosed readouts, both referenced within the review window (and both the latest data for each program; no additional new readouts were disclosed between the pre-quarter window start and the 10-Q filing date beyond these):

**Risto-cel — BEACON trial (sickle cell disease).** Originally presented at ASH Annual Meeting, December 2025; published in the New England Journal of Medicine April 1, 2026 issue (data cutoff date not separately specified in the 10-Q beyond the publication reference). Population: ~50 adults/adolescents with severe sickle cell disease, prior treatment with ≥1 disease-modifying agent with inadequate response/intolerance; open-label, single-arm (no concurrent comparator). Endpoints: safety (neutrophil/platelet engraftment), efficacy (change in severe VOCs, transfusion requirements, HbF levels, QoL). Results: mean HbF >60%, mean HbS reduction to <40%, pancellular HbF distribution; mean peripheral blood editing 67.4% at Month 6, 72.8% by Month 12; median time to neutrophil engraftment 17.5 days, platelet engraftment 19 days; 29% of patients required no platelet transfusions; one patient death (4 months post-infusion, respiratory failure, investigator-assessed as likely related to busulfan conditioning, not risto-cel); no investigator-reported severe VOCs post-engraftment. Single-arm, open-label — not a controlled comparison.

**BEAM-302 — Phase 1/2 trial (AATD).** Updated data announced March 2026; data cut-off February 10, 2026. Population: 29 patients dosed (Part A, lung disease, doses 15/30/60/75 mg and a 2×60 mg multi-dose cohort; Part B, liver disease, 30/60 mg), followed up to 18 months; 28 of 29 were efficacy-evaluable (one 60 mg Part B patient not yet evaluable at cutoff). Open-label, dose-escalation/expansion, no control arm. Endpoints: safety/tolerability, pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics (total/functional AAT, mutant Z-AAT reduction), efficacy. Results: well-tolerated safety profile up to 75 mg, no SAEs or DLTs as of cutoff; mean total AAT at steady state 16.1 µM (60 mg) and 14.4 µM (75 mg); mean Z-AAT reduction 84% (60 mg) and 79% (75 mg); mean M-AAT proportion 94% (60 mg) and 91% (75 mg); Part B liver-disease patients showed comparable efficacy. Company selected 60 mg as the optimal biological dose for the pivotal cohort, dosing of which began July 2026 (subsequent event, disclosed in the 10-Q filed Aug 4, 2026).

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management guidance (verbatim, Q2 2026 earnings release, Exhibit 99.1 to the 8-K filed 8/4/2026):** "Beam expects that its cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026, together with an additional $200 million expected to be drawn from the company's facility with Sixth Street, will fund anticipated operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into mid-2029, funding the company through the anticipated launch of risto-cel in SCD, execution of the BEAM-302 pivotal development plan in AATD, and clinical proof of concept for BEAM-304 in PKU."

**Calculated A — operating burn basis.** Cash + marketable securities at 6/30/2026 = $219,825K + $933,102K = $1,152,927K. Six-month operating cash outflow = $194,609K → average quarterly burn = $97,305K/quarter (194,609 ÷ 2). Runway = $1,152,927K ÷ $97,305K/quarter ≈ **11.9 quarters (~2.96 years)** from 6/30/2026, i.e., to roughly **Q2 2029**. This is broadly consistent with management's "mid-2029" guidance, before counting the additional $200 million draw management layers in.

**Calculated B — adjusted burn (debt/lease add-on).** The Sixth Street Credit Facility requires quarterly interest payments (effective rate 10.5% at 6/30/2026) but no principal repayment until the February 24, 2033 maturity date; interest payments are already embedded in the reported operating cash flows above, and operating lease payments are likewise already reflected in the operating section (change in operating lease ROU assets/liabilities). No separate scheduled debt amortization or lease obligation sits outside Calculated A, so Calculated B ≈ Calculated A, approximately **Q2 2029**.

**Pro forma.** Adding the additional $200 million Beam expects to draw under the Sixth Street facility's delayed-draw tranches (Delayed Draw A upon FDA acceptance of the risto-cel BLA, Delayed Draw B upon FDA approval of risto-cel — both pre-conditioned on regulatory events, not guaranteed or dated with certainty) to the $1,152,927K cash/securities balance gives pro forma liquidity of ~$1,352,927K, which at the $97,305K/quarter burn rate implies ~13.9 quarters (~3.48 years) from 6/30/2026, i.e. roughly **Q4 2029/Q1 2030** — consistent with, and somewhat beyond, the "mid-2029" guidance once the $200 million is actually drawn (fees excluded from this calculation).

Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:
- At-the-market (ATM) facility with Jefferies LLC: up to $1.1 billion aggregate gross proceeds authorized; $864.0 million sold to date through 6/30/2026 (none sold during Q2 2026), leaving ~$236 million of remaining capacity under the current agreement.
- Effective universal shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (filed February 2024, File No. 333-277427) covering common stock, preferred stock, debt securities, warrants and/or units in an indeterminate amount.
- Sixth Street Credit Facility: up to three additional $100 million delayed-draw tranches beyond the $200 million already contemplated in pro forma guidance (Delayed Draw upon revenue target, and a further $100 million subject to agreement among the Company and Lenders) — up to $500 million total facility size.
- Eli Lilly milestone stream: up to $350.0 million in potential development-stage payments, of which $50.0 million received through 6/30/2026 (remaining ~$300.0 million contingent on clinical/regulatory/alliance events).
- Bristol-Myers Squibb (Orbital acquisition) escrow: up to ~$26.3 million in additional cash consideration contingent on escrow releases (partial $0.5 million received in Q2 2026).
- Pfizer and Apellis/Biogen license and collaboration milestone/royalty potential (amounts not disclosed in filings reviewed).
- Company permitted under the Credit Facility's covenants to carry up to $400 million of unsecured convertible notes.
