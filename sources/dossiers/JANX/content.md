## 1. Profile

Janux Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: JANX) is headquartered in San Diego, California. It is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing immuno-oncology and immunology candidates on three proprietary platforms: Tumor Activated T Cell Engager (TRACTr), Tumor Activated Immunomodulator (TRACIr), and Adaptive Immune Response Modulator (ARM). Stage classification: **Early/Mid stage** — the lead program, JANX007, is in a Phase 1b trial (dose optimization/expansion); JANX014 and JANX011 are in Phase 1; JANX013 has not yet entered the clinic (planned 2H 2026); all other programs are preclinical. No approved products. Lead program: JANX007, a PSMA-targeted TRACTr T-cell engager for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), designed to minimize on-target healthy-tissue toxicity and improve pharmacokinetics versus earlier-generation T-cell engagers.

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management guidance (verbatim, Form 10-Q, Item 2, MD&A, filed 8/6/2026):** "Based on our current operating plan, we believe that our existing cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments, will be sufficient to meet our anticipated operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements through at least the next 12 months, following the date of this Quarterly Report." (This is the standard ASC 205-40 12-month going-concern assertion, not a multi-year horizon statement; the Q2 2026 earnings release headline separately calls the balance sheet "strong" without giving a specific runway endpoint date.)

**Calculated A — operating burn basis.** Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments at 6/30/2026 = $27,768K + $943,145K = $970,913K (matches the $970.9 million figure in the Q2 2026 earnings release). Six-month net cash provided by (used in) operating activities was a net inflow of $147K (not a burn) for H1 2026, versus a $40,837K outflow for H1 2025 — the H1 2026 figure is not representative of underlying cash burn because it was boosted by a one-time, largely non-recurring swing in deferred revenue (+$36,149K, driven by the new BMS upfront/milestone payments). A burn-rate calculation using the reported GAAP operating cash flow would therefore imply an essentially infinite (undefined) runway, which is not a meaningful planning figure. Using the **prior-year comparable period** as a proxy for underlying run-rate burn (H1 2025 net cash used in operations of $40,837K ÷ 2 quarters = $20,419K/quarter), runway would be $970,913K ÷ $20,419K/quarter ≈ **47.6 quarters (~11.9 years)** — again not representative, because it ignores the cash the company will need to deploy as its pipeline (JANX013 clinical entry, JANX007 Phase 1b expansion) scales up materially from H1 2025 spending levels.
**Given the distortion from deferred-revenue timing, the most decision-useful burn proxy here is loss from operations net of non-cash items**: six-month loss from operations of $65,903K, less $14,000K of BMS revenue already collected in cash (upfront/milestone, not matched to cash collection timing precisely) is not separable with the data disclosed; this tearsheet therefore reports both the GAAP figure and the caveat rather than computing a single calculated burn rate, per the instruction not to estimate to fill a gap.

**Calculated B — adjusted burn (debt/lease add-on).** Janux carries no interest-bearing debt. Operating lease liabilities (current $2,552K + non-current $18,438K = $21,990K total) are serviced through ordinary operating cash flows already reflected in Calculated A's inputs; no separate off-balance-sheet lease or debt service obligation exists. Calculated B is therefore not meaningfully different from Calculated A under either basis above.

**Pro forma.** No post-balance-sheet financing (equity raise, debt draw) was disclosed in the 10-Q's subsequent events or in any 8-K between 6/30/2026 and the as-of date (10/26/2026); no pro forma adjustment applies.

**Interpretation:** With $970.9 million in cash and short-term investments against six-month operating expenses of only $79.9 million (and recent net burn effectively offset by BMS collaboration cash receipts), Janux has a multi-year nominal runway by any conventional burn-rate measure, consistent with management's (deliberately conservative, GAAP-minimum) 12-month going-concern statement. A precise "quarters-to-zero-cash" figure is not disclosed by management and is not independently computed here because reported H1 2026 operating cash flow is not representative of sustainable run-rate burn (see above); this is flagged as "not disclosed in filings reviewed" for a specific calculated endpoint date, while qualitatively the company is extremely well funded relative to near-term spend.

- ATM Equity Offering Sales Agreement with BofA Securities, Inc.: up to $150.0 million aggregate offering price; no activity during the three or six months ended 6/30/2026 or 6/30/2025; full $150.0 million remained available as of 6/30/2026, under a Form S-3ASR shelf filed May 2024.

- Merck collaboration: up to $500.5 million per target (two targets already selected) in potential additional milestone payments, plus royalties on any resulting product sales — contingent on Merck's own development progress, timing not controlled by Janux.
