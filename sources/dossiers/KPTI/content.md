## 2. One-Minute Read

1. **Company is in active default and reliant on a day-to-day forbearance agreement:** Karyopharm did not pay a ~$15.8 million term-loan principal installment due September 10, 2026, has not paid cash interest on its 2028/2029 Convertible Notes since June 30, 2026, and the resulting Forbearance Agreement with lenders/noteholders has been extended twice (to October 22, 2026, then to November 1, 2026) — the company states that absent additional funding or a strategic transaction it "will be unable to continue as a going concern" and "may have to consider seeking protection under the bankruptcy laws."
2. **Q2 2026 total revenue was $33.4 million** (down from $37.9 million in Q2 2025), with U.S. XPOVIO net product revenue of $30.8 million (up from $29.7 million); full-year 2026 guidance of $130–150 million total revenue was reaffirmed as of the August 13, 2026 earnings release but was issued before the September 2026 default.
3. **Key late-stage pipeline catalyst in motion despite the financial crisis:** a supplemental NDA for selinexor + ruxolitinib in myelofibrosis was submitted August 31, 2026 under the Accelerated Approval pathway with Priority Review requested, based on positive Phase 3 SENTRY trial data (statistically significant spleen-volume response, promising overall-survival signal); FDA filing-acceptance decision expected Q4 2026.
4. **Phase 3 endometrial cancer trial failed its primary endpoint:** XPORT-EC-042 (announced July 30, 2026) missed statistical significance for progression-free survival in the mITT population (HR 0.76, one-sided p=0.0791; median PFS 12.75 vs 7.43 months), though a favorable trend was observed; company has deprioritized this indication to focus resources on myelofibrosis and multiple myeloma.
5. **Balance sheet shows deepening stockholders' deficit:** total stockholders' deficit widened to $(330.2) million at June 30, 2026 from $(292.9) million at December 31, 2025, driven by a $67.0 million Q2 net loss that included a $32.1 million non-cash charge from fair-value remeasurement of embedded derivatives and warrants tied to 2024/2025 refinancing transactions.

## 4. Clinical Trial Results Summary

- **Phase 3 SENTRY trial (myelofibrosis, NCT04562389), N=353, JAKi-naïve patients randomized 2:1 to selinexor+ruxolitinib vs. placebo+ruxolitinib.** Co-primary endpoints: SVR35 at week 24 and average change in Abs-TSS over 24 weeks. Results presented as a Late-Breaking Oral Presentation at ASCO 2026 and simultaneously published in the Journal of Clinical Oncology (data cut-off date not disclosed in the 8-K/press release reviewed); also presented at EHA 2026 (selected as one of the six best abstracts at the congress). Reported as demonstrating "rapid, deep and sustained spleen responses," a statistically significant improvement in SVR35 at week 24, a "promising" overall survival signal (OS is a pre-specified secondary endpoint; trial remains blinded for long-term follow-up, no crossover permitted), and reductions in variant allele frequency. This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled (vs. ruxolitinib alone) trial — the most rigorous design category.
- **Phase 3 XPORT-EC-042 trial (endometrial cancer, NCT05611931), N=257 total, mITT population n=236.** Randomized 1:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled. Topline results announced July 30, 2026 (data cut-off date not separately disclosed). Primary endpoint of PFS **not met** at a formal significance threshold: median PFS 12.75 months (selinexor) vs. 7.43 months (placebo); HR = 0.76 (95% CI: 0.51–1.12); one-sided p-value = 0.0791 (not significant at conventional one-sided alpha, e.g., 0.025 or 0.05 pre-specified boundary not disclosed but p=0.0791 did not cross the stated significance bar given the company's characterization as "did not meet its primary endpoint"). Safety profile consistent with selinexor's known profile; no new safety signals.
- **Phase 2 SENTRY-2 (myelofibrosis, NCT05980806):** 60 mg cohort (n=29) fully enrolled as of earlier in 2026; 40 mg cohort enrollment ongoing as of the August 13, 2026 press release. No efficacy data cut disclosed in this window; topline data for the 60 mg cohort expected H2 2026 (not yet reported as of October 23, 2026).
- **Phase 3 XPORT-MM-031/EMN29 (multiple myeloma, NCT05028348):** event-driven trial; patients continue to be followed for PFS events as of the Q2 2026 earnings release. No interim or topline data disclosed in this window; topline expected H2 2026.

No new trial result was disclosed for any program other than the EC-042 topline (negative) and the previously-reported SENTRY data reiterated/re-presented at conferences during the window; both are reported above with their actual, as-disclosed dates.

## 6. Runway Calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, from 10-Q Note 1 "Liquidity, Capital Resources and Going Concern"):**
> "As of June 30, 2026, we had $65.1 million of cash, cash equivalents, and investments and an accumulated deficit of $1.8 billion... Based on our current business plan and current capital resources, combined with the uncertainty regarding the availability of additional funding or other strategic alternatives and considering our debt service obligations and financial covenant to maintain minimum liquidity, we have concluded that there is substantial doubt regarding our ability to continue as a going concern within one year after the date the accompanying condensed consolidated financial statements are issued. We expect that our existing liquidity, including cash, cash equivalents, and investments together with anticipated cash flow from net product revenue and license and other revenue, will enable us to fund our current operating plans into September 2026... On September 10, 2026, a $15.8 million principal payment is due under our senior secured term loan facility; if this payment is made without additional financing or a waiver from our lenders, we expect that (i) our cash, cash equivalents and investments will fall below our $10.0 million minimum liquidity covenant, which would trigger a default on our term loan and (ii) we will not have sufficient resources to fund our operations following such payment... Absent additional funding or our ability to successfully complete one or more strategic transactions to extend our cash runway beyond September 10, 2026, we will be unable to continue as a going concern... we may have to consider seeking protection under the bankruptcy laws, liquidating our assets or ceasing our operations."

**Subsequent event update (verbatim from Sept. 11, 2026 8-K, Item 8.01, superseding the 10-Q guidance above):**
> "Based on its current operating plan and assuming that the Forbearance Agreement remains in effect through October 15, 2026, the Company expects that its existing liquidity, including cash, cash equivalents, and investments as well as cash flow from net product revenue and license and other revenue, will enable the Company to fund its current operating plans until October 15, 2026. Absent additional funding or entry into one or more strategic transactions to extend the Company's cash runway beyond October 15, 2026, the Company will be unable to continue as a going concern and may have to consider seeking protection under the bankruptcy laws, liquidating assets or ceasing operations."

The Forbearance Period was subsequently extended twice more (8-Ks filed October 14 and October 22, 2026) to November 1, 2026 — the runway, as of this writing (October 23, 2026), is effectively defined week-to-week by forbearance extensions rather than by a calculated cash runway.

**Calculated A — Operating burn basis (pre-default, as of 6/30/2026 balance sheet date):**
- H1 2026 net cash used in operating activities = $47.659 million over 6 months → average quarterly operating burn ≈ $23.8 million/quarter.
- Cash, cash equivalents and investments at 6/30/2026 = $65.1 million (per 10-Q Note 1; balance sheet shows $54,980 cash + $10,125 investments = $65,105, consistent).
- At the H1 2026 average operating burn rate alone, this would support ≈65.1 / 23.8 ≈ 2.7 quarters (~8 months) of operations — but this measure excludes debt service entirely and is therefore not the binding constraint; the binding constraint is Calculated B below.

**Calculated B — Including scheduled debt service (term loan amortization + cash interest on term loan and Notes), per the 10-Q's own disclosed schedule (Note 1, "Total estimated cash payments due under the financial instruments described in Note 10"):**

| Period | Total cash payments due (debt service only) |
|---|---|
| Q3 2026 (3 months ended 9/30/2026) | $25,972 thousand |
| Q4 2026 (3 months ended 12/31/2026) | $14,788 thousand |
| Q1 2027 (3 months ended 3/31/2027) | $14,507 thousand |
| Q2 2027 (3 months ended 6/30/2027) | $14,226 thousand |
| **Total, trailing 12 months from 6/30/2027** | **$69,493 thousand** |

Against $65.1 million of cash/investments at 6/30/2026 and no further financing, the Q3 2026 debt-service payment alone ($26.0 million) combined with ~2 months of ongoing operating burn (~$16 million) would have exhausted available liquidity before the $15.8 million Sept. 10 term-loan installment could be paid — which is exactly what occurred: the company defaulted on the September 10, 2026 payment and has not made other required interest payments since June 30, 2026. This is not a modeling exercise; it is the realized outcome disclosed in the Sept 11, Oct 14 and Oct 22, 2026 8-Ks.

**Pro forma (post-balance-sheet financing/defaults):**
- September 17, 2026: $20.0 million of forbearance fees paid entirely in newly issued 0% Series A Convertible Perpetual Preferred Stock (20,000 shares at $1,000 liquidation preference, convertible at $1.62/share) — this is a non-cash fee settlement, not new cash into the company; it does NOT extend the cash runway, it only avoids an immediate $20 million cash outflow for fees.
- No new third-party cash financing (equity raise, new debt, or asset sale) had closed as of October 23, 2026 per the filings reviewed; the company is in active, advisor-led (Centerview Partners) discussions on "financing opportunities and strategic alternatives," with no transaction terms disclosed.
- As of September 10, 2026 (8-K), outstanding balances were: term loan principal ≈$129.0 million; 2028 Notes ≈$15.6 million principal; 2029 Notes ≈$108.0 million principal; future royalty obligations under the Royalty Agreement ≈$113.5 million — a pro forma runway cannot be meaningfully calculated because the company's own forward liquidity is now governed entirely by week-to-week forbearance extensions (most recently to November 1, 2026) rather than by its own cash flow projections.

**Funding sources not counted in the runway math above:**
- At-the-market (ATM) "Open Market Sale Agreement" — actively used in H1 2026 (raised $20.2 million net); remaining capacity not disclosed in the 10-Q sections reviewed (not disclosed in filings reviewed).
- May/June 2026 private placement of common stock and warrants raised $30.0 million gross in H1 2026 financing activities (already reflected in the cash flow statement above, not an additional undrawn source).
- Outstanding liability-classified common stock warrants (fair value $19.8 million at 6/30/2026) — a derivative liability, not a source of incoming cash unless exercised for cash by holders.
- International partner milestone/royalty revenue from Menarini, Antengene, Neopharm and FORUS — contributes to "license and other revenue" (already in the income statement) but is not a committed lump-sum funding source and shrank in this period (Menarini's $15.0 million annual R&D reimbursement obligation expired December 31, 2025).
- Convertible Preferred Stock issued September 17, 2026 is a non-cash fee settlement (see above), not new capital raised.
- Any proceeds from a future equity raise or strategic/M&A transaction currently being pursued with Centerview Partners — undefined, no transaction announced as of October 23, 2026.
