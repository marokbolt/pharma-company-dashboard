## 1. Profile

Kura Oncology, Inc. (Nasdaq: KURA) is a commercial-stage, oncology-focused biopharmaceutical company headquartered in San Diego, California. It has one FDA-approved product — KOMZIFTI® (ziftomenib), a menin inhibitor for relapsed/refractory NPM1-mutated acute myeloid leukemia (AML), approved November 13, 2025 and launched commercially on November 21, 2025 — partnered globally with Kyowa Kirin. Beyond the approved indication, the company is running registration-directed Phase 3 trials (KOMET-017) to expand ziftomenib into frontline AML, and has an early-stage pipeline including darlifarnib (a farnesyltransferase inhibitor) in Phase 1 combination trials for solid tumors. Stage classification: **late-stage/commercial** (one approved and marketed product; registration-directed Phase 3 trials ongoing for label expansion; earlier-stage pipeline in Phase 1).

Source: 10-Q filed 2026-08-12 (period end 2026-06-30), Item 2 MD&A, "Overview" and "Our Product and Pipeline" sections.

## 2. One-minute read

- **Ziftomenib (KOMZIFTI) is FDA-approved and commercial**: approved November 13, 2025 for relapsed/refractory NPM1-mutated AML; the company generated **$14.9 million** in KOMZIFTI net product revenue for the six months ended June 30, 2026 (vs. $0 in the same period a year earlier, pre-approval). (10-Q, Item 2 MD&A)
- **Cash position fell sharply**: cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were **$519.0 million** as of June 30, 2026, down from **$667.2 million** at December 31, 2025 (cash $149.1M + ST investments $518.1M), driven by a six-month operating cash outflow of **$148.4 million**. (10-Q balance sheet and cash flow statement, and MD&A)
- **Management's own runway guidance is into Q4 2027** for the current operating plan, extending to first topline KOMET-017 results (2028) when combined with anticipated Kyowa Kirin payments of $180.0 million — a guidance window shorter than what Calculated A (operating-burn-only) implies under this skill's standard method (see Section 6). (10-Q, Item 2 MD&A, "Liquidity and Capital Resources")
- **Registration-directed Phase 3 program underway for frontline AML**: KOMET-017 (two independent, global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trials — combination with venetoclax/azacitidine in non-intensive patients, and combination with 7+3 induction chemotherapy in intensive patients) initiated September 2025; topline results from the intensive-chemotherapy MRD-negative CR endpoint anticipated in **2028**.
- **Window event — new CFO and a novel asset spin-out**: on September 8, 2026, the company appointed Jennifer Fulk as CFO (replacing interim arrangements) and formed subsidiary Caspian Therapeutics, Inc. to hold next-generation menin inhibitors for diabetes/cardiometabolic disease, which raised $50.0 million in Series A preferred financing from outside investors (Kura invested $4.3 million and retains ~49.2% of Caspian's capital stock) — a non-dilutive way to fund a non-oncology pipeline branch off-balance-sheet. (8-Ks filed 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09)

## 6. Runway calculations

**Management's own guidance (verbatim, 10-Q Item 2, "Liquidity and Capital Resources"):**
> "As of June 30, 2026, we had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $519.0 million. We believe that our cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments as of June 30, 2026 will be sufficient to fund our current operating plan into the fourth quarter of 2027. In addition, when combined with the anticipated $180.0 million in payments under the Kyowa License Agreement, we expect to have sufficient capital to advance our ziftomenib AML program through the first topline results from KOMET-017, anticipated in 2028."

**Calculated A — operating burn basis:**
- Six-month operating cash outflow = $148,403K → average quarterly operating burn = $148,403K / 2 = **$74,201.5K/quarter**
- Cash + short-term investments at June 30, 2026 = $70,242K + $448,783K = **$519,025K** ($519.0M, matching management's own figure)
- Quarters of runway = $519,025K / $74,201.5K ≈ **7.0 quarters** from June 30, 2026
- End point: June 30, 2026 + 7.0 quarters ≈ **Q1 2028**

Calculated A (≈Q1 2028) is slightly longer than management's own guidance (into Q4 2027). This is expected: management's "operating plan" guidance embeds planned spending increases (e.g., ramping SG&A/commercial infrastructure, Phase 3 enrollment costs) that a flat trailing-six-month average burn rate does not capture, and guidance is typically phrased conservatively. Both bases point to roughly 5–7 quarters of funded runway from the balance-sheet date without new financing.

**Calculated B — adjusted burn (operating burn + scheduled debt/lease payments):**
- Outstanding term debt (Hercules Loan Agreement): $4,217K current portion + $5,573K long-term = $9,790K total; interest-only until May 1, 2027, then monthly principal + interest until maturity November 2, 2027. Interest rate as of June 30, 2026: 9.15%.
- Current operating lease liabilities: $1,291K (within 12 months of June 30, 2026)
- Because the bulk of term-loan principal repayment falls inside the Calculated A window (interest-only ends May 2027, loan matures Nov 2027, inside the ~Q1 2028 runway endpoint), Calculated B is materially shorter than Calculated A. Using the full $9,790K of debt principal plus one year of lease payments (~$1,291K; lease maturity schedule not separately itemized in this excerpt) as additional near-term cash outflow not captured in the operating-burn average: incremental burn ≈ $11,081K, reducing the funded quarters by roughly 0.15 quarters. **Calculated B ≈ 6.9 quarters from June 30, 2026 ≈ Q1 2028** (not materially different from Calculated A given the debt balance is small relative to cash on hand).
- No minimum cash covenant was disclosed in the reviewed filings, so Calculated C (covenant-adjusted) is not applicable — **not disclosed in filings reviewed**.

**Pro forma:**
No post-balance-sheet-date financing event (equity offering, ATM sale, or debt draw) was disclosed in the 8-Ks filed in this window. The Caspian Therapeutics transaction (Sept 8, 2026) raised $50.0 million at the Caspian subsidiary level from outside investors, but Kura itself contributed $4.3 million into that entity (a cash outflow for Kura, not an inflow) and does not consolidate Caspian's $50.0 million proceeds onto Kura's own balance sheet in a way disclosed in these filings. **No pro forma adjustment is made to Kura's runway** based on filings reviewed.

**Latest-quarter run-rate sensitivity:** The 10-Q discloses operating cash flow only on a six-month, year-to-date basis; the Q2-alone (three-month) operating cash outflow is **not disclosed separately in filings reviewed**, so a quarter-only sensitivity check (required when it differs from the year-to-date average by more than 20%) could not be computed.

**Funding sources NOT counted in the runway math above:**
- ATM Facility: up to $150.0 million in common stock may be sold at the company's discretion; **no shares have been sold under it** as of June 30, 2026 (10-Q, Item 2 MD&A)
- Hercules Loan Agreement: up to $125.0 million total facility was available; only a $10.0 million tranche was drawn, and the remaining tranches expired undrawn — no further borrowing capacity remains under this agreement
- Kyowa Kirin License Agreement: anticipated **$180.0 million** in additional payments (milestone/other, not yet received as of the filing), plus the company has already received or expects to receive $597.1 million in aggregate under the agreement as of June 30, 2026 — these are collaboration-contingent, not held as cash
- Warrants/options outstanding: not quantified in the sections reviewed — **not disclosed in filings reviewed** (would require the Stockholders' Equity note, not separately fetched in this review)
- Caspian Therapeutics equity stake: Kura retains ~49.2% of Caspian's outstanding capital stock after the $50.0 million Series A financing; any future value is not cash and is not counted here
